## 摘要
背景：牛结核病（bTB）是由牛分枝杆菌（Mycobacterium bovis）引起的一种慢性传染病，给全球畜牧业造成重大经济损失，并可导致包括人类在内的其他哺乳动物患结核病（TB）。肺泡巨噬细胞是病原体在感染早期攻击的宿主细胞。尽管它们在控制感染中起着至关重要的作用，但宿主-病原体相互作用的确切性质以及调节感染结果的遗传和表观遗传因素仍不清楚。结果：本研究利用转录组学（RNA-seq）、染色质可及性（ATAC-seq）和染色质构象（ChIP-seq）分析，研究了胞内分枝杆菌感染对牛肺泡巨噬细胞（bAM）转录组和表观基因组的影响。研究重点是牛分枝杆菌感染，但也对结核分枝杆菌（人类结核病的主要病因）、牛分枝杆菌卡介苗（BCG，疫苗株）和伽马射线照射（灭活）的牛分枝杆菌进行了平行比较分析。RNA-seq、ChIP-seq 和 ATAC-seq 数据的整合揭示了染色质可及性和组蛋白修饰景观的协调重塑，这支撑了响应感染的关键免疫和代谢途径的转录激活。通过与牛分枝杆菌感染易感性的全基因组关联研究（GWAS）进行整合分析，鉴定了包括 ERBB4、LRCH1、MRTFA 和 RNPC3 在内的候选基因，强调了这些调节网络的功能相关性。结论：我们的结果表明，牛分枝杆菌驱动了 bAM 表观基因组的广泛重编程，这与结核分枝杆菌复合体（MTBC）其他成员引起的反应不同。这一多组学比较的结果为关键响应基因的功能提供了新见解，并支持了病原体驱动的牛宿主巨噬细胞表观遗传重编程是牛分枝杆菌存活关键的假设。该研究还确定了分子靶点，可为旨在增强牛对牛结核病抗性的基因组育种策略提供参考。

## Abstract
Background: Bovine tuberculosis (bTB) is a chronic infectious disease primarily caused by Mycobacterium bovis, which inflicts significant economic losses on the global livestock industry worldwide and can also cause tuberculosis (TB) disease in other mammalian species, including humans. Alveolar macrophages are the host cells targeted by the pathogen during the early stages of infection. While they play a crucial role in controlling infection, the exact nature of the host-pathogen interaction and the genetic and epigenetic factors that modulate infection outcome remain poorly understood. Results: Here, we used transcriptomics (RNA-seq), chromatin accessibility (ATAC-seq), and chromatin configuration (ChIP-seq) analyses to examine the effects of intracellular mycobacterial infection on the bovine alveolar macrophage (bAM) transcriptome and epigenome. The primary focus was M. bovis infection, but we also conducted parallel comparative analyses using M. tuberculosis (the primary cause of human TB - hTB), M. bovis BCG (the vaccine strain), and gamma-irradiated (killed) M. bovis. Integration of RNA-seq, ChIP-seq, and ATAC-seq data revealed coordinated remodelling of chromatin accessibility and histone modification landscapes underpinning transcriptional activation of key immune and metabolic pathways in response to infection. The identification of candidate genes, including ERBB4, LRCH1, MRTFA, and RNPC3, through integrative analysis with a genome-wide association study (GWAS) for M. bovis infection susceptibility underscores the functional relevance of these regulatory networks. Conclusions: Our results demonstrate that M. bovis drives extensive reprogramming of the bAM epigenome, distinct from the responses elicited by other members of the M. tuberculosis complex (MTBC). The results of this multi-omics comparison provide new insights into the function of pivotal response genes and support the hypothesis that pathogen-driven epigenetic reprogramming of the bovine host macrophage is key to M. bovis survival. It also identifies molecular targets that may inform genome-enabled breeding strategies to enhance resilience to bTB in cattle.
