## 摘要
目的：狼疮性肾炎（LN）是系统性红斑狼疮（SLE）的一种严重并发症，会导致进行性肾纤维化和功能下降。为了开发有效的治疗策略，需要了解免疫细胞与基质细胞之间的相互作用。本研究调查了人类 LN 中巨噬细胞与成纤维细胞相互作用的图谱，并在小鼠模型中验证了这些发现。方法：我们利用来自 AMP-SLE 队列的 156 例人类 LN 活检样本和 30 例健康对照的单细胞 RNA 测序（scRNAseq）数据，以及 6 例 LN 患者活检样本的空间转录组学数据，表征了不同的成纤维细胞亚群及其与肾脏巨噬细胞的相互作用。通过小鼠模型的体外共培养研究，进一步明确了这些相互作用的功能后果。结果：我们鉴定了两个肌成纤维细胞亚群：富集在肾小管间质的促炎亚群（Myofib1）和位于肾小球的纤维化/重塑亚群（Myofib2），两者均与组织学慢性指数相关。空间转录组学揭示了不同的共定位模式，其中 Myofib1 与激活的常驻巨噬细胞（RM）亚群相互作用，而 Myofib2 与肾小球浸润的疾病相关巨噬细胞相互作用。体外共培养研究表明，肾炎 RM 促进促炎、重塑型成纤维细胞表型，从而损害伤口愈合并驱动 Myofib1 样基因程序，而疾病相关巨噬细胞则产生具有修复能力失调的促纤维化成纤维细胞。细胞间通讯分析确定了介导这种跨界通讯的关键配体-受体相互作用，包括 Spp1/整合素、Sema4/PlexinB 和 NAMPT/INSR。结论：我们的数据揭示了 LN 中巨噬细胞-成纤维细胞相互作用在空间和功能上的异质性图谱。这些发现增进了我们对 LN 肾纤维化发生的理解，突出了可能作为预防慢性肾损伤治疗靶点的特定纤维化-炎症回路。

## Abstract
ObjectivesLupus nephritis (LN) is a severe complication of systemic lupus erythematosus (SLE), leading to progressive renal fibrosis and functional decline. Understanding the interplay between immune cells and stromal cells is needed to develop effective therapeutic strategies. Here, we investigated the landscape of macrophage-fibroblast interactions in human LN and validated these findings in mouse models.

MethodsWe characterized distinct fibroblast subsets and their interactions with renal macrophages using single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNAseq) of 156 human LN biopsies and 30 healthy controls from the AMP-SLE cohort, and spatial transcriptomics of biopsies from 6 LN patients. In vitro co-culture studies using mouse models were performed to further define functional consequences of these interactions.

ResultsWe identified two myofibroblast subsets: a pro-inflammatory subset (Myofib1) enriched in the tubulointerstitium, and a fibrotic/remodeling subset (Myofib2) in glomeruli, both correlating with the histologic chronicity index. Spatial transcriptomics revealed different colocalization patterns, with Myofib1 interacting with activated resident macrophage (RM) subsets and Myofib2 with glomerular infiltrating disease-associated macrophages. In vitro co-culture studies demonstrated that nephritic RMs promote a pro-inflammatory, remodeling fibroblast phenotype that impairs wound healing and drives a Myofib1-like gene program, whereas disease-associated macrophages generated profibrotic fibroblasts with dysregulated reparative capacity. Cell-cell communication analyses identified key ligand-receptor interactions mediating this crosstalk, including Spp1/integrins, Sema4/PlexinB, and NAMPT/INSR.

ConclusionsOur data reveal a spatially and functionally heterogeneous landscape of macrophage-fibroblast crosstalk in LN. These findings advance our understanding of renal fibrogenesis in LN, highlighting specific fibro-inflammatory circuits that may represent therapeutic targets to prevent chronic renal damage.
