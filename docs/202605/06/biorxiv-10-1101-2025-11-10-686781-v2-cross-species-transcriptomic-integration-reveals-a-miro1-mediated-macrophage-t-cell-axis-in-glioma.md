## 摘要
线粒体调节因子在肿瘤微环境（TME）内对免疫信号的影响日益受到关注。在免疫抑制限制治疗效果的胶质瘤中，我们研究了靶向线粒体蛋白 MIRO1 如何改变 TME。我们将体内接受 MIRO1 结合化合物治疗的小鼠胶质瘤单核 RNA 测序与离体接受相同化合物处理的人类胶质瘤切除样本的大体 RNA 测序相结合。跨物种转录组整合揭示了 TME 中对 MIRO1 响应的程序。在共有靶点中，我们鉴定出 PARP11/Parp11 是胶质瘤中持续上调的基因，而在人类和小鼠胶质瘤中，接受 MIRO1 结合化合物治疗后该基因均下调。细胞间通讯分析显示，一个特定的巨噬细胞簇（MAC1）表现出强劲的 Parp11 和 Pdl1（编码 PD-L1）表达，向 CD8+ 细胞毒性 T 细胞发送免疫抑制信号，并可能接收来自另一个巨噬细胞簇（MAC4）的前列腺素 E2 信号。靶向 MIRO1 消除了这种细胞回路并减少了肿瘤细胞群。我们的研究为理解线粒体-免疫交叉对话提供了一个转录组框架，并提名 MIRO1-PARP11 作为大脑免疫功能障碍的潜在效应轴。

## Abstract
Mitochondrial regulators are increasingly recognized for their influence on immune signaling within the tumor microenvironment (TME). In glioma, where immunosuppression limits therapeutic efficacy, we investigate how targeting the mitochondrial protein MIRO1 alters the TME. We combine single-nucleus RNA sequencing of murine gliomas treated in vivo with a MIRO1-binding compound and bulk RNA sequencing of human glioma resections treated with the same compound ex vivo. Cross-species transcriptomic integration reveals a MIRO1-responsive program in the TME. Among shared targets, we identify PARP11/Parp11 as a consistently upregulated gene in glioma that is downregulated following MIRO1-binding compound treatment in both human and mouse gliomas. Cell-cell communication analysis shows that a specific cluster of macrophages (MAC1), which exhibits robust Parp11 and Pdl1 (encoding PD-L1) expression, sends immunosuppressive signals to CD8+ cytotoxic T cells, and may receive prostaglandin E2 signals from another cluster of macrophages (MAC4). Targeting MIRO1 eliminates this cell circuitry and reduces the tumor cell population. Our study provides a transcriptomic framework for understanding mitochondria-immune crosstalk and nominates MIRO1-PARP11 as a potential effector axis of brain immune dysfunction.
