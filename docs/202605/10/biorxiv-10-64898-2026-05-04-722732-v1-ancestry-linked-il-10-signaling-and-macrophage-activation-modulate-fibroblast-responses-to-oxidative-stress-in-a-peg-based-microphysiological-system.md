## 摘要
与祖先相关的免疫差异影响纤维化风险，然而，纤维化相关通路在个体间如何变化仍不清楚。成纤维细胞是参与纤维化的主要细胞类型，其反应受细胞因子信号传导和巨噬细胞激活的影响。这些通路在个体间的差异程度，以及与祖先相关的免疫差异如何影响纤维化风险，目前仍知之甚少。在本研究中，利用基于聚乙二醇 (PEG) 的水凝胶微生理系统来模拟氧化应激后的成纤维细胞-巨噬细胞相互作用，并使用匹配的巨噬细胞和血清整合供体特异性免疫信号。与欧洲裔个体相比，自述为非洲裔的个体表现出更高的单核细胞 CCL4 表达、更低的单核细胞 OXER1 表达以及升高的血清 IL-10 水平。在水凝胶内，氧化应激降低了成纤维细胞的占比，同时诱导了 Ki67 和 p16 的表达。外源性 TGF-beta 增加了成纤维细胞的占比和 3 型胶原蛋白的产生，但并未独立增加 alpha-SMA。整合供体特异性巨噬细胞和血清后发现，来自欧洲裔个体的培养物表现出更高的成纤维细胞 alpha-SMA 和 p16 表达。对 IL-10 的药理学抑制进一步增加了 alpha-SMA 的表达，特别是在非洲裔来源的培养物中，这表明 IL-10 是限制成纤维细胞激活的关键保护信号。该水凝胶系统为剖析个体间免疫差异和识别与祖先相关的纤维化风险潜在机制提供了一个平台。

## Abstract
Ancestry-associated immune differences influence fibrosis risk, however, how fibrosis-associated pathways vary across individuals remains poorly understood. Fibroblasts are a main cell type involved in fibrosis. The fibroblast response is shaped by cytokine signaling and macrophage activation. The extent to which these pathways vary across individuals, and how ancestry-associated immune differences influence fibrosis risk, remains poorly understood. Here, a poly(ethylene glycol) (PEG)-based hydrogel microphysiological system was leveraged to model fibroblast-macrophage interactions following oxidative stress and to integrate donor-specific immune signals using matched macrophages and serum. Individuals of self-reported African ancestry exhibited higher monocyte expression of CCL4, lower monocyte expression of OXER1, and increased serum IL-10, compared to individuals of European ancestry. Within the hydrogel, oxidative stress reduced fibroblast prevalence while inducing Ki67 and p16. Exogenous TGF-beta increased fibroblast prevalence and collagen 3 production but did not independently increase alpha-SMA. Incorporating donor-specific macrophages and serum revealed that cultures from individuals of European ancestry demonstrated higher fibroblast alpha-SMA and p16 expression. Pharmacologic inhibition of IL-10 further increased alpha-SMA, particularly in African ancestry-derived cultures, identifying IL-10 as a key protective signal limiting fibroblast activation. This hydrogel system provides a platform for dissecting inter-individual immune variation and identifying mechanisms underlying ancestry-associated fibrosis risk.
