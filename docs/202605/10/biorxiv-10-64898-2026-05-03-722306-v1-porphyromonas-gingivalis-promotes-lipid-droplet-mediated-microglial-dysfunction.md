## 摘要
越来越多的证据支持牙周炎与阿尔茨海默病（AD）之间存在强相关性，但连接这些疾病的机制仍不明确。在包括 AD 在内的神经退行性疾病中，小胶质细胞通常表现为脂滴（LD）积累增加、激活增强以及功能受损。在本研究中，我们探讨了关键牙周致病菌——牙龈卟啉单胞菌（Pg）是否会促进小胶质细胞中的脂滴积累并破坏其功能。我们发现，Pg 感染诱导了 BV2 小胶质细胞以及 Pg 感染的 App KI 小鼠小胶质细胞中显著的脂滴积累。这种由 Pg 驱动的脂滴堆积与活性氧（ROS）产生增加、吞噬能力受损以及激活状态改变密切相关。值得注意的是，使用甘油三酯合成抑制剂对脂滴进行药理学抑制，可有效逆转 Pg 诱导的脂滴积累，减轻 ROS 的产生，并恢复吞噬功能，这强调了脂质代谢在调节小胶质细胞功能中的关键作用。这些发现表明，Pg 感染驱动了小胶质细胞的脂质失调和功能受损，并支持了牙周感染促进小胶质细胞脂滴积累的模型。这种代谢改变通过氧化应激和吞噬功能受损的自我强化循环加剧了小胶质细胞功能障碍，从而可能加速 AD 的进展。

## Abstract
Growing evidence supports a strong association between periodontitis and Alzheimer's disease (AD), yet the mechanisms linking these conditions remain poorly defined. In neurodegenerative disorders, including AD, microglia are often characterized by increased lipid droplet (LD) accumulation, heightened activation, and impaired functional capacity. In this study, we examined whether Porphyromonas gingivalis (Pg), a keystone periodontal pathogen, promotes LD accumulation in microglia and disrupts their function. We found that Pg infection induces robust LD accumulation in BV2 microglial cells and in microglia from Pg-infected App KI mice. This Pg-driven LD buildup was closely associated with elevated reactive oxygen species (ROS) production, impaired phagocytic ability, and altered activation. Notably, pharmacological inhibition of LDs with a triglyceride synthesis inhibitor effectively reversed Pg-induced LD accumulation, mitigated ROS production, and restored phagocytic function, underscoring the critical role of lipid metabolism in regulating microglial function. These findings demonstrate that Pg infection drives microglial lipid dysregulation and functional impairment and support a model in which periodontal infection promotes LD accumulation in microglia. This metabolic alteration exacerbates microglial dysfunction through a self-reinforcing cycle of oxidative stress and impaired phagocytosis that may accelerate AD progression.
