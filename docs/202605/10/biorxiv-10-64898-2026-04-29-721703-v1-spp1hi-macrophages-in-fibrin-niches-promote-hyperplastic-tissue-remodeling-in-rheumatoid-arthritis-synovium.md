## 摘要
在慢性炎症性疾病中，适应不良的组织重塑是由驻留细胞、免疫浸润物和细胞外基质之间复杂的相互作用驱动的。在自身免疫性疾病类风湿关节炎（RA）中，滑膜组织经历大规模扩张，形成侵蚀软骨和骨骼的侵袭性血管翳。介导这种侵袭性生长的机制尚未完全明确。通过对患者组织进行空间转录组学分析，我们在滑膜组织衬里表面附近以及 SPP1hi 巨噬细胞相邻处检测到大量的增殖成纤维细胞。值得注意的是，这些滑膜衬里区域还具有明显的血凝块形成蛋白——纤维蛋白沉积特征。虽然 SPP1hi 巨噬细胞在表型上类似于驱动肺和肝纤维化的促纤维化巨噬细胞，但这些龛位缺乏标志纤维化的致密且高度有序的胶原蛋白。在功能上，我们发现 SPP1hi 巨噬细胞能够降解并吞噬纤维蛋白基质，并促进成纤维细胞增殖。由于纤维蛋白在伤口愈合过程中为组织新生提供瞬时基质，这些数据支持了一种涉及 SPP1hi 巨噬细胞、成纤维细胞和粘附在滑膜组织外表面的纤维蛋白基质的增生性组织向外生长模型。虽然目前的 RA 疗法主要旨在抑制促炎反应，但我们的研究结果为针对促再生通路和 SPP1hi 巨噬细胞提供了理论依据。单句总结：RA 滑膜纤维蛋白沉积中的 SPP1hi 巨噬细胞促进组织增生。

## Abstract
In chronic inflammatory diseases, maladaptive tissue remodelling is driven by a complex interplay of resident cells, immune filtrates and the extracellular matrix. In the autoimmune disorder rheumatoid arthritis (RA), synovial tissue undergoes assive expansion to form an invasive pannus that drives the erosion of cartilage and bone. The mechanisms mediating this ggressive growth are incompletely defined. Using spatial transcriptomics profiling of patient tissue, we detected an bundance of proliferating fibroblasts near the synovial tissue lining surface and adjacent to SPP1hi macrophages. Notably, ese synovial lining regions were also distinctly marked by deposits of the clot-forming protein fibrin. While the SPP1hi acrophages phenotypically resemble pro-fibrotic macrophages that drive lung and liver fibrosis, these niches were devoid f the dense highly ordered collagen that marks fibrosis. Functionally, we found that SPP1hi macrophages degrade and hagocytose fibrin matrices and promote fibroblast proliferation. As fibrin provides transient matrices for de novo tissue eneration in the context of wound healing, these data support a model of hyperplastic tissue outgrowth involving SPP1hi acrophages, fibroblasts and fibrin matrices adhered to the exterior synovial tissue surface. While current RA therapies rimarily aim to dampen pro-inflammatory responses, our findings provide rationale for targeting pro-generative pathways nd SPP1hi macrophages.
