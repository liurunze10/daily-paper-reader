## 摘要
背景：免疫检查点阻断（ICB）在约半数晚期黑色素瘤患者中实现了持久缓解，但其余患者产生耐药性的机制基础尚未完全明确。AXL酪氨酸激酶已成为候选的耐药介质，然而AXL抑制剂在未筛选患者群体中的临床开发结果呈现异质性，这表明细胞类型和肿瘤微环境背景可能关键性地决定了治疗反应。方法：我们利用公开的单细胞RNA测序数据集和癌症基因组图谱（TCGA）表征了ICB耐药黑色素瘤中不同细胞类型的AXL表达。在YUMM1.7 PD-1耐药同系黑色素瘤模型中，评估了三种药理学性质不同的AXL抑制剂（华法林、bemcentinib和卡博替尼）作为单药及与抗PD-1疗法联合使用的体内疗效。通过抗CSF1R和抗F4/80耗竭策略确定了肿瘤相关巨噬细胞（TAM）的背景依赖性。利用免疫福斯特共振能量转移（iFRET）技术对肿瘤切片中功能性PD-L1:PD-1检查点相互作用进行了定量。通过40重Luminex免疫分析表征了极化RAW264.7巨噬细胞中TAM分泌组的重编程。结果：单细胞分析显示，ICB耐药黑色素瘤中的AXL表达主要定位于TAM而非肿瘤细胞，且AXL+ TAM分布在M1样和M2样表型区室中。AXL抑制显著减轻了肿瘤负荷，并在体内与抗PD-1疗法产生协同作用；然而，疗效在耗竭单核细胞衍生的髓系区室（抗CSF1R）后消失，而在耗竭组织驻留TAM（抗F4/80）后增强，从而确立了TAM背景依赖性。iFRET揭示了抗PD-1治疗的耐药肿瘤中PD-L1:PD-1相互作用效率反常增加，这是一种可通过iFRET检测但无法通过PD-L1表达检测的功能性耐药特征，而AXL联合治疗可逆转这一现象。体外分泌组分析表明，联合治疗以极化背景依赖的方式重编程巨噬细胞分泌组，在M2样巨噬细胞中放大包括IL-6和GM-CSF在内的促炎细胞因子，同时选择性地抑制T细胞趋化因子的产生。结论：这些发现确立了AXL是ICB耐药黑色素瘤中TAM驻留的免疫靶点，其治疗相关性由肿瘤免疫微环境（TiME）中的巨噬细胞组成而非肿瘤细胞的AXL表达决定。TAM极化背景是基于AXL抑制剂联合策略的候选分层轴，而iFRET测量的检查点相互作用为监测耐药性和治疗反应提供了PD-L1表达的功能性补充。

## Abstract
Background: Immune checkpoint blockade (ICB) achieves durable responses in approximately half of patients with advanced melanoma, but the mechanistic basis for resistance in the remaining patients remains incompletely defined. AXL tyrosine kinase has emerged as a candidate resistance mediator, yet clinical development of AXL inhibitors has yielded heterogeneous results in unselected patient populations, suggesting that cell-type and tumor microenvironment context may critically govern therapeutic response. Methods: We characterized AXL expression across cell types in ICB-resistant melanoma using publicly available single-cell RNA sequencing datasets and The Cancer Genome Atlas (TCGA). In vivo efficacy was assessed in the YUMM1.7 PD-1-resistant syngeneic melanoma model using three pharmacologically distinct AXL inhibitors (warfarin, bemcentinib, and cabozantinib) as monotherapy and in combination with anti-PD-1 therapy. Tumor-associated macrophage (TAM) context-dependency was established using anti-CSF1R and anti-F4/80 depletion strategies. Functional PD-L1:PD-1 checkpoint interactions were quantified in tumor sections by immune Forster Resonance Energy Transfer (iFRET). TAM secretome reprogramming was characterized by 40-plex Luminex immunoassay in polarized RAW264.7 macrophages. Results: AXL expression in ICB-resistant melanoma was predominantly localized to TAMs rather than tumor cells by single-cell analysis, with AXL+ TAMs distributed across both M1-like and M2-like phenotypic compartments. AXL inhibition significantly reduced tumor burden and synergized with anti-PD-1 therapy in vivo; however, efficacy was abolished by depletion of the monocyte-derived myeloid compartment (anti-CSF1R) and enhanced by depletion of tissue-resident TAMs (anti-F4/80), establishing TAM-context dependency. iFRET revealed a paradoxical gain of PD-L1:PD-1 interaction efficiency in anti-PD-1-treated resistant tumors, a functional resistance signature detectable by iFRET but not by PD-L1 expression that was reversed by AXL combination therapy. In vitro secretome profiling demonstrated that combination therapy reprograms macrophage secretome in a polarization-context-dependent manner, amplifying pro-inflammatory cytokines including IL-6 and GM-CSF in M2-like macrophages while selectively dampening T cell chemokine production. Conclusions: These findings establish AXL as a TAM-resident immune target in ICB-resistant melanoma whose therapeutic relevance is governed by tumor-immune micronenvironment (TiME) macrophage composition rather than tumor cell AXL expression. TAM polarization contexture represents a candidate stratification axis for AXL inhibitor-based combination strategies, and iFRET-measured checkpoint interaction offers a functional complement to PD-L1 expression for monitoring resistance and response.
