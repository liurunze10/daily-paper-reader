## 摘要
半胱氨酸蛋白质组在压力期间本质上调节细胞的适应性。蛋白质组中的半胱氨酸通过可逆修饰来避免因氧化而损失。可逆的半胱氨酸修饰通过调节底物蛋白的瞬时激活，有益地保留了细胞的应激反应机制。半胱氨酸酰基化（或S-酰基化）就是这样一种被广泛记录的可逆蛋白质修饰。炎症反应引起的S-酰基化变化通过动态决定小胶质细胞（大脑中主要的免疫反应细胞）中压力缓冲（底物）半胱氨酸蛋白的利用，促进了免疫分区大脑的塑性。然而，小胶质细胞中半胱氨酸酰基化的炎症反应性尚未在大规模上得到详细测量。因此，我们通过实施基于MLCC的比较化学蛋白质组学工作流程，观察了培养的人类（HMC3）小胶质细胞中蛋白质的差异（定量）S-酰基化。同时，我们通过免疫细胞化学和蛋白质组学实验，表征了用广谱S-酰基化抑制剂2-溴棕榈酸酯处理HMC3细胞的分子后果。在用病原体相关分子模式（PAMPs，如LPS或IFNy）处理小胶质细胞后，我们还通过实时时空追踪和共聚焦显微镜鉴定了依赖于S-酰基化的小胶质细胞炎症反应。最后，通过对人类死后脑组织的蛋白质组学研究，我们证实了精神分裂症（一种与氧化还原相关的脑部疾病）的主要致病蛋白会发生炎症反应性S-酰基化。本报告提供了首个关于小胶质细胞中通过炎症反应性酰基化差异处理的半胱氨酸蛋白的全面库。它还概述了关键NRF2抗氧化剂（包括过氧化物酶，特别是PRDX2，以及谷胱甘肽合成酶GSS）的炎症反应性S-酰基化，作为小胶质细胞早期炎症和氧化应激的生化特征。更重要的是，为本研究设计的创新蛋白质信息学工作流程可以可靠地重新用于识别许多复杂疾病的双功能诊疗蛋白质标志物。

## Abstract
Cysteine proteome essentially regulates cellular adaptability during stress. Proteomic cysteines are reversibly modified to avoid loss through oxidation. Reversible cysteine-modifications beneficially preserve cellular stress-response mechanisms by regulating the transient activation of substrate proteins. Cysteine acylation (or s-acylation) is one such extensively documented reversible protein modification. Inflammation-responsive changes in s-acylation facilitate the plasticity of immune-partitioned brains by dynamically determining the  utilization of stress-buffering (substrate) cysteine-proteins in microglia, the primary immune-reactive brain cells.

However, the inflammation-responsiveness of cysteine-acylation in microglia is not elaborately measured at a large-scale. Therefore, we observed the differential (quantitative) s-acylation of proteins in cultured human (HMC3) microglia by implementing comparative MLCC-based chemoproteomic workflows. Parallelly, we characterized the molecular consequences of HMC3 cell treatment with 2-bromopalmitate, a general s-acylation inhibitor, by performing immunocytochemistry and proteomic experiments. We also identified the s-acylation-dependent microglial inflammatory responses through live spatiotemporal tracking and confocal microscopy, following treatment of microglia with PAMPs (LPS or IFNy). Finally, from our proteomic studies of postmortem human brain tissues, we confirmed major protein-causals of schizophrenia, a redox-related brain disorder, to undergo inflammation-responsive s-acylation.

This report provides a first comprehensive library of cysteine-proteins differentially processed through inflammation-responsive acylation in microglia. It also outlines the inflammation-responsive s-acylation of key NRF2-antioxidants, including peroxiredoxins (especially PRDX2) and glutathione synthetase (GSS), as a biochemical signature of early-stage inflammation and oxidative-stress in microglia. More importantly, the innovative proteo-informatic workflows designed for this research work can be reliably repurposed to identify bifunctional theragnostic protein markers of many complex disorders and diseases.
