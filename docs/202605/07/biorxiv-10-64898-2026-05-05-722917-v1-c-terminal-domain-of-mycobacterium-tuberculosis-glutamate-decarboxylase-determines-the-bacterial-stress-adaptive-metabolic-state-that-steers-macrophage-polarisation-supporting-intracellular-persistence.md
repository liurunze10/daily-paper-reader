## 摘要
结核分枝杆菌（Mtb）通过代谢适应在肺泡巨噬细胞的恶劣环境中生存，但特定的酶驱动代谢状态如何将应激适应与宿主免疫调节联系起来尚不清楚。利用LC-MRM/MS代谢物谱分析和代谢建模，我们此前发现在适应氧化和酸性应激期间，通过GABA支路产生的γ-氨基丁酸（GABA）水平升高。在此，我们证明谷氨酸脱羧酶（GadB）产生GABA的应激响应酶效率不仅减轻了酸性和氧化应激，还驱动巨噬细胞向M2表型极化，将其作用从生物能量学扩展到了宿主-病原体相互作用。我们确定分枝杆菌GadB的C-末端结构域（CTD）是关键调节因子。酶动力学和荧光光谱显示，CTD缺失消除了催化效率（kcat/Km）和底物亲和力（Ka）的pH依赖性调节。在氧化应激下，CTD在不改变底物亲和力的情况下提高了催化效率，表明其具有特定条件的调节作用。相应地，在体外酸性和氧化条件下，产生较高GABA且ROS降低约40%的Mtb::MtbGadB菌株比Mtb::MtbGadBΔCt生长得更好。这些生化差异影响了宿主免疫，其中高产GABA的Mtb::MtbGadB菌株感染诱导了M2极化，表现为CD86降低、RNS减少、内体成熟受限（由Rab5/Rab7比率和基于Lysotracker的共聚焦显微镜指示），以及与低产GABA的Mtb::MtbGadBΔCt相比，TNF-α、IL-12p70和IFN-γ释放减少。因此，Mtb::MtbGadBΔCt表现出更高的清除率。我们得出结论，通过实现GadB的pH依赖性活性，CTD协调了质子消耗、ROS减少和GABA介导的免疫调节。人类与Mtb GAD中CTD截然不同的结构特征突显了CTD可作为病原体特异性、宿主兼容疗法的选择性药物靶点。

## Abstract
Mycobacterium tuberculosis (Mtb) adapts metabolically to survive hostile conditions within alveolar macrophages, but whether specific enzyme-driven metabolic states of Mtb link stress adaptation to host immunomodulation remains unclear. Using LC-MRM/MS metabolite profiling and metabolic modeling, we previously identified elevated {gamma}-aminobutyric acid (GABA) production via the GABA shunt during adaptation to oxidative and acidic stress. Here, we show that stress-responsive enzymatic efficiency of glutamate decarboxylase (GadB) in producing GABA, not only mitigates acidic and oxidative stress but also drives macrophage polarisation towards the M2 phenotype, extending its role beyond bioenergetics into host-pathogen cross-talk. We identify the C-terminal domain (CTD) of mycobacterial GadB as a key regulator. Enzyme kinetics and fluorescence spectroscopy revealed that CTD deletion abolishes pH-dependent regulation of catalytic efficiency (kcat/Km) and substrate affinity (Ka). Under oxidative stress, the CTD enhances catalytic efficiency without altering substrate affinity, suggesting condition-specific roles. Accordingly, in vitro, Mtb::MtbGadB with higher GABA and ~40% lower ROS outgrew Mtb::MtbGadB{Delta}Ct under acidic and oxidative conditions. These biochemical differences influenced host immunity, wherein infection with high-GABA-producing Mtb::MtbGadB strain induced M2 polarisation, with decreased CD86, reduced RNS, limited endosomal maturation, as indicated by Rab5/Rab7 ratio and Lysotracker-based confocal microscopy, and decreased TNF-, IL-12p70, and IFN-{gamma} release compared to low-GABA-producing Mtb::MtbGadB{Delta}Ct. Consequently, Mtb::MtbGadB{Delta}Ct showed higher clearance. We conclude that by enabling pH-dependent activity of GadB, the CTD orchestrates proton consumption, ROS reduction, and GABA-mediated immunomodulation. Distinct structural features of CTDs in human versus Mtb GADs highlight the CTD as a selective drug target for pathogen-specific, host-compatible therapies.
