## 摘要
外周血（PB）是临床应用中调节性T细胞（Tregs）的主要来源，但目前尚未对PB Tregs与组织Tregs进行系统比较，其定义特征仍不清楚。本研究鉴定出一群具有较低自我反应性的循环偏向性PB Treg群体。尽管PB Tregs在体外的抑制能力较弱，但它们对危险相关核苷酸ATP和NAD+高度敏感，这与高ARTC2.2/CD38比例有关。ATP/NAD+触发的PB Tregs凋亡转化促进了巨噬细胞的胞葬作用（efferocytosis），并选择性地抑制了IFN-γ驱动的炎症极化，同时保留了IL-10相关程序并增强了清除受体Mertk，这表明胞葬作用介导的巨噬细胞重编程有助于病变控制。与该模型一致，在缺乏T细胞的动脉粥样硬化易感小鼠中，转移PB Tregs可有效限制斑块生长和坏死核心形成。我们在小鼠和人类中均鉴定出对ATP/NAD+超敏感的PB Treg亚群，并提供了与该亚群在动脉粥样硬化斑块内被优先胞葬一致的证据。

## Abstract
Peripheral blood (PB) is the predominant source of regulatory T cells (Tregs) for clinical applications, yet PB Tregs have not been systematically compared with tissue Tregs and their defining properties remain poorly understood. Here we identify a circulation-biased PB Treg population with reduced self-reactivity. Although PB Tregs are less suppressive in vitro, they are hypersensitive to the danger-associated nucleotides ATP and NAD+, linked to a high ARTC2.2/CD38 ratio. ATP/NAD+-triggered apoptotic conversion of PB Tregs promotes macrophage efferocytosis and selectively dampens IFN-{gamma}-driven inflammatory polarization while preserving IL-10-associated programs and enhancing the scavenger receptor Mertk, suggesting that efferocytosis-mediated macrophage reprogramming contributes to lesion control. Consistent with this model, in atherosclerosis-prone mice lacking T cells, PB Treg transfer effectively limits plaque growth and necrotic core formation. We identify an ATP/NAD+-hypersensitive PB Treg fraction in both mice and humans and provide evidence consistent with preferential efferocytosis of this subset within atherosclerotic plaques.
