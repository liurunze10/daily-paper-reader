## 摘要
氧化化学是许多内源性信号通路的基础，但在活细胞中作为调节蛋白质功能的可编程策略仍未得到充分利用。在此，我们通过将光敏剂与特定的细胞内环境耦合，建立了遗传编码的氧化化学作为一种可调控框架，用于调节多种蛋白质。利用 miniSOG 产生活性氧（ROS），我们证明受控的细胞内氧化会增强氧化还原报告蛋白 HyPerRed 的荧光，并激活氧化还原敏感的 TRP 离子通道，其中 TRPA1 和 TRPV1 反应强烈，而 TRPV4 则没有。通路选择性清除剂揭示了可溶性靶点和膜靶点与不同氧化过程的差异化耦合，支持了基于环境而非统一氧化扰动的选择性。调节强度和动力学可通过光照参数、表达比例和亚细胞定位进行定量调节，其中膜靶向增强了与膜效应器的耦合。最后，将 miniSOG 融合靶向至 TRPV1 以及在人成纤维细胞中调节内源性 TRPA1，将该方法扩展到了蛋白质近端和天然细胞环境。总之，这些结果将遗传编码的氧化化学定位为一种多功能且具有空间组织性的模式，用于在定义的运行机制内工程化活细胞中的蛋白质功能。

## Abstract
Oxidative chemistry underlies many endogenous signaling pathways but remains underutilized as a programmable strategy for regulating protein function in living cells. Here we establish genetically encoded oxidative chemistry as a tunable framework for modulating diverse proteins by coupling a photosensitizer to defined intracellular contexts. Using miniSOG to generate reactive oxygen species (ROS), we show that controlled intracellular oxidation increases the fluorescence of the redox reporter HyPerRed and activates redox-sensitive TRP ion channels, with strong responses in TRPA1 and TRPV1 but not TRPV4. Pathway-selective scavengers reveal differential coupling of soluble and membrane targets to distinct oxidative processes, supporting selectivity by context rather than uniform oxidative perturbation. Modulation strength and kinetics are quantitatively tunable through illumination parameters, expression ratios, and subcellular localization, with membrane targeting enhancing coupling to membrane effectors. Finally, fusion targeting of miniSOG to TRPV1 and modulation of endogenous TRPA1 in human fibroblasts extend this approach to protein-proximal and native cellular settings. Together, these results position genetically encoded oxidative chemistry as a versatile and spatially organized modality for engineering protein function in living cells within a defined operating regime.
