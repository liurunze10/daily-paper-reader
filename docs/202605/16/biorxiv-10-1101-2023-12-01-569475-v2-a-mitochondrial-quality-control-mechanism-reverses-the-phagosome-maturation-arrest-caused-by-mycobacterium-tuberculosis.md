## 摘要
由结核分枝杆菌（Mtb）诱导的吞噬体成熟停滞（PMA）是帮助其逃避巨噬细胞抗菌反应的经典手段。小GTP酶RAB7从Mtb吞噬体中的排除导致了PMA。在此，我们报告了一种意想不到的机制，该机制触发了线粒体质量控制（MQC）与吞噬体成熟途径之间的交叉对话，从而逆转了PMA。CRISPR介导的p62/SQSTM1耗竭（p62KD）似乎并不影响线粒体质量。p62KD细胞能够限制Mtb的生长，这是由日益增强的氧化环境和增加的溶酶体靶向所触发的。Mtb的溶酶体靶向是由增强的TOM20+线粒体衍生囊泡（MDVs）生物发生促进的，这是一种关键的MQC机制。在p62KD细胞中，TOM20+-MDVs的生物发生依赖于MIRO1/MIRO2，并以RAB7依赖的方式被递送到溶酶体进行降解。在p62KD细胞受感染后，TOM20+-MDVs被广泛靶向至Mtb吞噬体，无意中促进了RAB7的招募、PMA的逆转以及Mtb的溶酶体靶向；该表型在p62/SQSTM1敲除细胞中也得到了复制。在p62KD细胞中触发MQC崩溃进一步降低了Mtb的存活率，表明了氧化还原机制与溶酶体介导机制之间的协作。MQC与抗菌途径之间的交叉对话可被用于开发针对宿主的抗结核疗法。

## Abstract
Phagosome maturation arrest (PMA) imposed by Mycobacterium tuberculosis (Mtb) is a classic tool that helps Mtb evade macrophage anti-bacterial responses. The exclusion of RAB7, a small GTPase, from Mtb-phagosomes causes PMA. Here, we report an unexpected mechanism that triggers crosstalk between the mitochondrial quality control (MQC) and the phagosome maturation pathways that reverses the PMA. CRISPR-mediated p62/SQSTM1 depletion (p62KD) does not appear to impact mitochondrial quality. The p62KD cells are restrictive to Mtb growth, triggered by an increasingly oxidative environment and increased lysosomal targeting. The lysosomal targeting of Mtb is facilitated by enhanced TOM20+ mitochondria-derived vesicles (MDVs) biogenesis, a key MQC mechanism. In p62KD cells, TOM20+-MDVs biogenesis is MIRO1/MIRO2-dependent and gets delivered to lysosomes for degradation in a RAB7-dependent manner. Upon infection in p62KD cells, TOM20+-MDVs get extensively targeted to Mtb-phagosomes, inadvertently facilitating RAB7 recruitment, PMA reversal and lysosomal targeting of Mtb; the phenotype also replicated in p62/SQSTM1 knockout cells. Triggering MQC collapse in p62KDcells further diminishes Mtb survival, signifying cooperation between redox- and lysosome-mediated mechanisms. The MQC-anti-bacterial pathway crosstalk could be exploited for host-directed anti-tuberculosis therapies.
