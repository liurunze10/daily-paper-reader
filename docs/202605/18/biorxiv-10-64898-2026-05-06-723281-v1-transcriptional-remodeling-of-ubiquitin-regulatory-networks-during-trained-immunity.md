## 摘要
背景：训练免疫是先天免疫细胞的一种持久性功能重编程，其特征是在二次挑战时反应性增强。虽然代谢重连和表观遗传重塑是该过程公认的特征，但泛素介导的翻译后调节所起的作用仍不明确。方法：我们对来自单核细胞、巨噬细胞和外周血单核细胞（PBMC）的公开人类转录组数据集进行了综合分析，这些细胞暴露于已确定的训练刺激（β-葡聚糖、卡介苗 [BCG] 和血红素-β-葡聚糖）并随后进行二次刺激。分析了一组具有明确免疫功能的去泛素化酶（DUBs）和 E3 泛素连接酶的差异表达情况。通过基因本体论（GO）和 KEGG 通路富集分析评估了独立数据集之间的高阶收敛性。结果：在多个训练免疫模型中，我们发现了泛素修饰酶的可重复转录重塑。在重新刺激后，USP25、OTUB1 和 TRIM25 持续上调，而几种染色质和细胞因子调节相关的 DUBs（包括 USP3、USP4、USP7、USP16、MYSM1 和 USP38）则下调。以 RPMI 重新刺激的对照组进行归一化处理后，许多与激活相关的信号有所减弱；然而，USP25 仍保持持续升高，表明其是一个稳定的训练相关特征。通路富集分析独立证明了各数据集中泛素相关功能类别的显著参与，支持了泛素调节网络的协调重组。结论：这些发现表明，泛素-蛋白酶体系统的选择性转录重塑是训练免疫的一个反复出现的特征。将泛素信号整合到现有的代谢-表观遗传框架中，扩展了先天免疫记忆的概念模型，并表明泛素修饰酶作为调节变阻器，塑造了免疫反应的幅度和稳定性。未来的功能和蛋白质组学研究需要确定这些转录特征是否直接介导了训练免疫表型。

## Abstract
BackgroundTrained immunity is a durable functional reprogramming of innate immune cells characterized by enhanced responsiveness upon secondary challenge. While metabolic rewiring and epigenetic remodeling are well-established features of this process, the contribution of ubiquitin-mediated post-translational regulation remains poorly defined.

MethodsWe performed an integrative analysis of publicly available human transcriptomic datasets derived from monocytes, macrophages, and PBMCs exposed to established training stimuli ({beta}-glucan, Bacillus Calmette-Guerin [BCG], and hemin-{beta}-glucan) followed by secondary stimulation. A curated panel of deubiquitinating enzymes (DUBs) and E3 ubiquitin ligases with established immune functions was analyzed for differential expression. Gene Ontology (GO) and KEGG pathway enrichment analyses were conducted to evaluate higher-order convergence across independent datasets.

ResultsAcross multiple trained immunity models, we identified reproducible transcriptional remodeling of ubiquitin-modifying enzymes. USP25, OTUB1, and TRIM25 were consistently upregulated following restimulation, whereas several chromatin- and cytokine-regulatory DUBs--including USP3, USP4, USP7, USP16, MYSM1, and USP38--were downregulated. Normalization to RPMI-restimulated controls reduced many activation-associated signals; however, USP25 remained persistently elevated, suggesting a stable training-associated signature. Pathway enrichment analysis independently demonstrated significant engagement of ubiquitin-related functional categories across datasets, supporting coordinated reorganization of ubiquitin regulatory networks.

ConclusionThese findings identify selective transcriptional remodeling of the ubiquitin- proteasome system as a recurring feature of trained immunity. Integrating ubiquitin signaling into the established metabolic-epigenetic framework expands the conceptual model of innate immune memory and suggests that ubiquitin-modifying enzymes function as modulatory rheostats shaping immune amplitude and stability. Future functional and proteomic studies are required to determine whether these transcriptional signatures directly mediate trained immunity phenotypes.
