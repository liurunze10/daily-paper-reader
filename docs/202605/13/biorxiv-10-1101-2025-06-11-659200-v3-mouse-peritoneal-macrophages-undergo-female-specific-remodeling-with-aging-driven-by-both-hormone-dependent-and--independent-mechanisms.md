## 摘要
衰老是一个复杂的过程，其特征是生理功能的逐渐下降。免疫功能受生物性别的强烈影响，同时影响先天性和适应性反应。在本研究中，我们调查了年龄和性别对小鼠腹腔免疫细胞的影响，并确定巨噬细胞是受影响最显著的细胞类型。巨噬细胞作为先天免疫系统的核心组成部分，在维持稳态和感染反应中发挥着关键作用。我们发现，衰老诱导了小鼠腹腔巨噬细胞组学图谱的性别特异性重塑，并伴随着雌性特异性的衰老相关功能重塑（如吞噬作用降低、糖酵解增加）。我们表明，循环雌激素水平的衰老相关变化可能驱动了雌性特异性巨噬细胞衰老相关变化的某些方面，特别是衰老相关的吞噬功能下降。通过利用我们的多组学数据集，我们鉴定了转录因子，其雌性特异性的年龄调节可能驱动腹腔巨噬细胞表型的雌性特异性衰老相关变化。有趣的是，Irf2 的下调足以重现雌性腹腔巨噬细胞衰老表型的某些方面，包括糖酵解增加。从机制上讲，Irf2 表达降低导致其与其靶基因（包括编码己糖激酶——糖酵解限速酶的 Hk3）的结合减少。我们的研究结果支持了这样一种观点，即衰老通过激素依赖性和非依赖性机制导致小鼠腹腔巨噬细胞的雌性特异性重塑。

## Abstract
Aging is a complex process characterized by a progressive decline in physiological functions. Immune function is strongly influenced by biological sex, affecting both innate and adaptive responses. Here, we investigated the effects of age and sex on mouse peritoneal immune cells and identified macrophages as the top affected cell type. Macrophages, as central components of the innate immune system, play critical roles in homeostasis maintenance and infection response. We found that aging induces sex-specific remodeling of mouse peritoneal macrophage  omic landscapes, which is accompanied by female-specific age-related functional remodeling (i.e. decreased phagocysis, increased glycolysis). We show that age-related changes in circulating estrogen levels likely drive aspects of female-specific macrophage age-related changes, specifically age-related phagocytosis decline. By leveraging our multi-omic dataset, we identify transcription factors whose female-specific age-regulation may drive female-specific age-related changes in peritoneal macrophage phenotypes. Interestingly, Irf2 downregulation was sufficient to recapitulate aspects of female peritoneal macrophage aging phenotypes, including increased glycolysis. Mechanistically, decreased Irf2 expression leads to decreased binding to its target genes, including Hk3, which encodes hexokinase (the rate-limiting enzyme of glycolysis). Our findings support the notion that aging leads to female-specific remodeling of mouse peritoneal macrophages through hormone-dependent and -independent mechanisms.
