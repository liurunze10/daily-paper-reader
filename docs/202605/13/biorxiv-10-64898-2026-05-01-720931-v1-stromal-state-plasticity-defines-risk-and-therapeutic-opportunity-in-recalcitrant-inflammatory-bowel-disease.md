## 摘要
尽管免疫靶向疗法不断增加，炎症性肠病（IBD）仍是一种反复发作、治疗耐药的疾病，其特征是进行性组织损伤和炎症。我们建立了一个前瞻性队列，整合了基质生物样本库、功能表型分析、跨队列基准测试和结局建模，以定义与疾病相关的细胞状态。来自34名健康个体、溃疡性结肠炎和克罗恩病患者的结肠肌成纤维细胞被解析为两种主要状态：炎症性肌成纤维细胞（IMFs）和静息态肌成纤维细胞（QMFs）。招募时 IMF 的优势地位预示了疾病的进展，在随访期间，尽管加强了治疗，内镜严重程度恶化的几率仍增加了约4.6倍，从而将早期基质生物学与临床终点联系起来。与 QMFs 不同，IMFs 表现出衰老相关分泌表型，损害了上皮干性、屏障完整性和先天免疫适应性。基于状态引导的优先级排序确定了 EDNRB 拮抗作用是一种高置信度的基质干预手段，可在正交实验和跨物种研究中逆转致病表型。结局模拟表明，通过 EDNRB 拮抗作用实现的基质状态可逆性是一个精准治疗轴，可将难治风险降低约96.4%，并将治疗耐药重新定义为一种可逆的基质状态。在这项工作中，Tindle 等人确定了控制难治性 IBD 的可逆基质状态，并提出将致病性肌成纤维细胞的精准重编程作为一种新的治疗策略。

## Abstract
Inflammatory bowel diseases (IBD) remain a relapsing, treatment-refractory disorder marked by progressive tissue injury and inflammation despite expanding immune-targeted therapies. We established a prospective cohort integrating stromal biobanking, functional phenotyping, cross-cohort benchmarking, and outcome modeling to define disease-anchored cellular states. Colonic myofibroblasts from 34 individuals spanning health, ulcerative colitis, and Crohns disease resolved into two dominant states: inflammatory (IMFs) and quiescent (QMFs) myofibroblasts. IMF predominance at recruitment forecasted progressive disease, increasing odds of worsening endoscopic severity despite therapy escalation by [~]4.6 during follow-up, thereby linking early stromal biology to clinical endpoints. Unlike QMFs, IMFs exhibited a senescence-associated secretory phenotype that impaired epithelial stemness, barrier integrity, and innate immune fitness. State-guided prioritization identified EDNRB-antagonism as a high-confidence stromal intervention, reversing pathogenic phenotypes across orthogonal assays and species. Outcome simulation positioned stromal-state reversibility by EDNRB-antagonism as a precision axis, reducing odds of recalcitrance by [~]96.4% and reframing treatment resistance as a reversible stromal state.
