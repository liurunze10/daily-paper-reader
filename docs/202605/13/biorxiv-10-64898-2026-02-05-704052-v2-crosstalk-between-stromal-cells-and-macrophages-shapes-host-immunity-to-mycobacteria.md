## 摘要
肉芽肿是分枝杆菌感染中定义疾病特征的异质细胞组织结构。它们发挥着多重作用，从遏制病原体到引起组织破坏。在本研究中，我们在 C57BL/6 小鼠中建立了一个成熟的腹膜肉芽肿模型，以研究分枝杆菌感染期间的动态细胞间相互作用，包括作为重要发病部位的浆膜腔内的长期免疫改变。我们发现分枝杆菌寄居在基质细胞中，基质细胞通过产生趋化因子和集落刺激因子 1 积极调节局部组织环境并塑造巨噬细胞反应。慢性感染诱导基质细胞持续重编程并向专门的类免疫状态多样化，包括将分枝杆菌主动转移至巨噬细胞以及显著的干扰素反应。因此，基质细胞获得了免疫调节特性，并支持病原体处理、单核细胞招募和巨噬细胞成熟，从而在肉芽肿形成及对分枝杆菌的免疫反应中发挥决定性作用。研究亮点：一种新型腹膜分枝杆菌感染模型揭示了成熟肉芽肿中的异质细胞通讯；分枝杆菌感染持久地重塑了作为重要发病部位的浆膜腔的免疫架构；基质细胞充当分枝杆菌宿主细胞并获得免疫效应功能；基质细胞通过招募骨髓源性单核细胞并与其直接沟通，共同组织组织中的宿主-病原体界面。

## Abstract
Granulomas are disease-defining heterocellular tissue structures in mycobacterial infections. They play a multifaceted role ranging from containing the pathogen to causing tissue destruction. Here, we established a mature peritoneal granuloma model in C57BL/6 mice to investigate the dynamic cell-cell interactions during mycobacterial infection, including long-term immune alterations in serous cavities as important sites of disease manifestation. We found that mycobacteria reside in stromal cells, which actively modulate the local tissue environment and shape macrophage responses, particularly through formation of chemokines and colony-stimulating factor 1. Chronic infection induces sustained reprogramming and diversification of stromal cells toward specialized, immune-like states, including active transfer of mycobacteria to macrophages and a pronounced interferon response. Consequently, stromal cells acquire immunoregulatory properties and support pathogen handling, monocyte recruitment and macrophage maturation, thereby playing a decisive role in granuloma formation and thus in the immune response to mycobacteria.
