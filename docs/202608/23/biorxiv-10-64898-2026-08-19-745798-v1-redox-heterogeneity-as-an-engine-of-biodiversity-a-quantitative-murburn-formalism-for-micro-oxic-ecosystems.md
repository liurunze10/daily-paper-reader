## 摘要
生物多样性通常在波动的微氧环境中达到峰值，如海洋最低氧区界面、根际聚合体、沉积物、微生物席和肠道粘液层。然而，经典的生态学理论未能充分解释为何中等氧张力会反复促进物种共存与多样化。本文中，我们提出了一种 murburn 生态学形式体系，其中氧气不仅作为代谢底物，还通过部分还原以及可扩散反应物种（DRS）和氧化还原中间体的形成，充当动态氧化还原异质性的生成器。通过将海洋、肠道、土壤和水生界面生态系统的实证观察与反应-扩散框架相结合，我们证明了中等氧张力能自然地使自由基场异质性最大化，并产生动态变化的适应度景观。数值模拟展示了在无外部生态位划分的情况下，微氧区内自发的共存、生物多样性极大值、局部多样化以及共存的稳定性。进一步的模拟表明，水生大型动物通过重构氧梯度并产生生态系统尺度的扩散氧化还原架构（ESDRA），间接增强了生物多样性。该框架提出，波动的氧化还原界面在不同生物尺度上充当了具有高适应性更替潜力的生态区。

## Abstract
Biodiversity frequently peaks in fluctuating micro-oxic environments such as marine oxygen minimum zone interfaces, rhizospheric aggregates, sediments, microbial mats, and gut mucus layers. Yet, classical ecological theories do not adequately explain why intermediate oxygen tensions repeatedly favor coexistence and diversification. Herein, we propose a murburn ecological formalism wherein oxygen acts not merely as a metabolic substrate but as a generator of dynamic redox heterogeneity through partial reduction and diffusible reactive species (DRS) and redox-intermediates formation. Integrating empirical observations from marine, gut, soil, and aquatic-interface ecosystems with a reaction-diffusion framework, we show that intermediate oxygen tensions naturally maximize radical-field heterogeneity and produce dynamically shifting fitness landscapes. Numerical simulations demonstrate spontaneous coexistence, biodiversity maxima within micro-oxic zones, localized diversification, and coexistence stabilization without externally imposed niche partitioning. Additional simulations suggest that aquatic macrofauna indirectly enhance biodiversity by restructuring oxygen gradients and generating ecosystem-scale diffusional redox architectures (ESDRA). The framework proposes that fluctuating redox interfaces function as potential ecological zones of elevated adaptive turnover across biological scales.
