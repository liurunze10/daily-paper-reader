## 摘要
人多能干细胞衍生的类肠器官在疾病建模、药物筛选和再生医学方面具有巨大潜力。然而，传统的类肠器官主要由上皮细胞组成，规模较小，且缺乏重现炎症性肠病（IBD）和结直肠癌（CRC）等肠道疾病病理生理学所需的多细胞复杂性。在此，我们报道了由诱导多能干细胞（iPSCs）开发的厘米级、纯三维自组装类人肠道器官（IOs），其涵盖了包括上皮、间充质、平滑肌、神经元、免疫细胞和血管在内的多种组织谱系。这些类器官在第100天以上实现功能成熟，表现出节律性的类蠕动收缩；到第147天，它们显示出包括管腔、类隐窝结构、杯状细胞和平滑肌在内的组织学结构。重要的是，神经-肌肉谱系首次在纯三维培养系统中自发且自主地产生，无需任何外部刺激（如电、化学或机械刺激），并成熟形成功能性神经肌肉接头，完全通过体外培养（无需异种移植）驱动肉眼可见的、模拟肠道运动的类蠕动收缩。第115天的单细胞RNA测序在四大主要谱系中鉴定出12种细胞亚型，重现了发育中人类肠道的细胞多样性。利用该平台，我们建立了LPS/IFN-γ诱导的IBD模型，重现了包括上皮破坏、免疫细胞浸润和IL-6升高在内的关键病理特征。转录组分析证实了NF-κB和JAK2-STAT3通路的激活、多模式细胞死亡以及免疫招募机制，均与临床IBD病理一致。此外，我们开发了培养7天和21天的肠癌模型，显示出异常增生，以及展示了抗炎功效的益生菌共培养系统。总之，这些结果确立了厘米级类肠器官作为一个生理相关的多细胞平台，用于模拟肠道疾病和评估治疗干预。

## Abstract
Human pluripotent stem cell-derived intestinal organoids hold great promise for disease modeling, drug screening, and regenerative medicine. However, conventional intestinal organoids are predominantly epithelial, small in scale, and lack the multicellular complexity required to recapitulate the pathophysiology of intestinal disorders such as inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) and colorectal cancer (CRC). Here, we report the development of Centimeter-Scale, purely 3D self-organized human intestinal organoids (IOs) from induced pluripotent stem cells (iPSCs) that encompass multiple tissue lineages, including epithelium, mesenchyme, smooth muscle, neurons, immune cells, and vasculature. These organoids achieve functional maturation by day 100+, exhibiting rhythmic peristaltic-like contractions, and by day 147 they display histological structures including lumens, crypt-like architecture, goblet cells, and smooth muscle. Importantly, for the first time, the neuro-muscle lineages arise spontaneously and autonomously in a purely 3D culture system, without any external stimulation (e.g., electrical, chemical, or mechanical), and mature to form functional neuromuscular junctions, driving macroscopically visible peristaltic-like contractions that mimic intestinal motility entirely through in vitro culture, without any xenotransplantation. Single-cell RNA sequencing at day 115 identified 12 cell subtypes across four major lineages, recapitulating the cellular diversity of the developing human intestine. Using this platform, we established an LPS/IFN-{gamma}-induced IBD model that recapitulated key pathological features, including epithelial disruption, immune cell infiltration, and IL-6 elevation. Transcriptomic analysis confirmed activation of the NF-{kappa}B and JAK2-STAT3 pathways, multi-modal cell death, and immune recruitment machinery, all consistent with clinical IBD pathology. Furthermore, we developed intestinal cancer models at 7 and 21 days showing abnormal hyperplasia, and a probiotic co-culture system demonstrating anti-inflammatory efficacy. Together, these results establish Centimeter-Scale intestinal organoids as a physiologically relevant, multicellular platform for modeling intestinal diseases and evaluating therapeutic interventions.
