## 摘要
目的：类风湿关节炎（RA）患者的循环单核细胞已处于炎症激活的预启动状态，但其疾病内在特征尚未得到系统表征。鉴于代谢在塑造免疫细胞功能中的重要作用，我们旨在利用无偏见的多组学方法，确定这种预启动状态的代谢基础，并探究这些变化是否在不同的激活状态下持续存在。方法：对来自 RA 患者和匹配健康供体的外周血 CD14+ 单核细胞在未分化状态（M0）以及分化为经典激活的 M(IFNy+LPS) 和替代激活的 M(IL-4) 巨噬细胞（随后进行急性脂多糖 LPS 刺激）后进行了分析。开展了代谢组学（非靶向 LC-MS/MS）、转录组学（RNA-seq）和蛋白质组学（无标记 LC-MS/MS）分析。通过加权基因共表达网络分析、差异分析、基因集富集分析、多组学因子分析和代谢通量建模对数据进行了全面分析。结果：RA 单核细胞在不同激活状态下表现出稳定的疾病驱动特征。代谢组、转录组和蛋白质组数据的整合揭示了代谢-分泌耦合的意外趋同，包括核苷酸和氧化还原代谢物的耗竭、线粒体和翻译通路的下调，以及分泌装置的重塑（包括顺式高尔基体组分的缺失）。一致地，代谢建模预测糖基化通量降低，从而将代谢变化与分泌能力的改变联系起来。结论：RA 单核细胞呈现出一种稳定的、疾病内在的状态，且在不同激活条件下持续存在。多组学数据识别出一种关联的代谢和分泌缺陷，糖基化能力降低是其潜在的功能后果。这种代谢-分泌耦合是 RA 单核细胞功能障碍的一个定义性特征，也是潜在的治疗靶点。

## Abstract
Objectives: Circulating monocytes from rheumatoid arthritis (RA) patients are pre-primed for inflammatory activation, but their disease-intrinsic features have not been systematically characterized. Given the important role of metabolism in shaping immune cell function, we aimed to determine how this pre-primed state is underpinned metabolically and whether these changes persist across different activation states, using an unbiased multi-omics approach. Methods: Peripheral blood CD14+ monocytes from RA patients and matched healthy donors were analyzed in an undifferentiated state (M0) and after differentiation into classically activated M(IFNy+LPS) and alternatively activated M(IL-4) macrophages, followed by acute lipopolysaccharide (LPS) stimulation. Metabolomic (untargeted LC-MS/MS), transcriptomic (RNA-seq), and proteomic (label-free mass LC-MS/MS) profiling were performed. Data was comprehensively analyzed by weighted gene correlation network analysis, differential analysis, gene set enrichment analysis, multi-omics factor analysis and metabolic flux modeling. Results: RA monocytes exhibited a stable disease-driven signature across activation states. Integration of metabolomic, transcriptomic and proteomic data revealed an unexpected convergence on metabolic-secretory coupling, with depletion of nucleotide and redox metabolites, downregulation of mitochondrial and translational pathways, and remodeling of the secretory apparatus, including loss of cis-Golgi components. Consistently, metabolic modeling predicted reduced glycosylation fluxes, connecting metabolic changes to altered secretory capacity. Conclusions: RA monocytes adopt a stable, disease-intrinsic state that persists across activation conditions. Multi-omics data identify a linked metabolic and secretory defect, with reduced glycosylation capacity as a potential functional consequence. This metabolic-secretory coupling represents a defining feature of RA monocyte dysfunction and a potential therapeutic target.
