## 摘要
吞噬作用是巨噬细胞和树突状细胞用于吞噬颗粒的一种专门的内吞过程，它需要协调的信号级联反应、细胞骨架重塑以及抗菌机制的组装以消除病原体。在 Fc γ 受体 (FcgR) 介导的吞噬过程中，吞噬杯底部的动态肌动蛋白去聚合为内质网-质膜 (ER-PM) 膜接触位点 (MCS) 的形成创造了许可条件。我们证明了内质网驻留蛋白酪氨酸磷酸酶 PTP1B 在吞噬过程中定位于新形成或扩大的 ER-PM MCS，并使 Syk 脱磷酸。利用包含 MAPPER、STIM1 和 E-Syts 在内的 MCS 驻留蛋白进行全内反射荧光 (TIRF) 显微镜观察，我们展示了肌动蛋白清除允许内质网蛋白接近质膜。PTP1B 在肌动蛋白清除区与 FcgR 共定位，并与吞噬信号传导的关键介质 Syk 发生物理相互作用。PTP1B 的缺失导致持续的 Syk 高磷酸化，但不影响吞噬作用。然而，PTP1B 缺陷细胞在 NADPH 氧化酶 2 (NOX2) 介导的超氧化物产生方面表现出 3 倍的增加。通过无偏见蛋白质组学，我们鉴定出接头蛋白 Shc1 是连接 Syk 磷酸化与 NOX2 激活的关键中间体。吞噬过程中的 Shc1 磷酸化依赖于 Src 家族激酶和 Syk，而 SHC1 的基因消融使超氧化物产生减少了 40%。原位邻位连接技术 (PLA) 显示，在吞噬过程中，PTP1B 缺陷细胞中 Shc1-p47phox 的相互作用增强。这些发现确立了一个 SFK-Syk-Shc1-NOX2 信号轴，PTP1B 在内质网与正在形成的吞噬体之间的 MCS 处对其进行负调节，为吞噬过程中的抗菌反应提供了新的机制见解。

## Abstract
Phagocytosis is a specialized endocytic process used by macrophages and dendritic cells to engulf particles, which requires coordinated signaling cascades, cytoskeletal remodeling, and assembly of antimicrobial machinery to eliminate pathogens. During Fc gamma receptor (FcgR)-mediated phagocytosis, dynamic actin depolymerization at the base of the phagocytic cup creates permissive conditions for endoplasmic reticulum-plasma membrane (ER-PM) membrane contact sites (MCS) to form. We demonstrate that the ER-resident protein tyrosine phosphatase PTP1B localizes to newly formed or expanded ER-PM MCS during phagocytosis and dephosphorylates Syk. Using TIRF microscopy with MCS residents, including MAPPER, STIM1, and E-Syts, we show that actin clearance allows ER proteins to approach the plasma membrane. PTP1B colocalizes with FcgR in actin-cleared zones and physically interacts with Syk, a critical mediator of phagocytic signaling. Loss of PTP1B led to sustained Syk hyperphosphorylation without affecting phagocytosis. However, the PTP1B-deficient cells showed a 3-fold increase in NADPH oxidase 2 (NOX2)-mediated superoxide production. Using unbiased proteomics, we identified the adapter protein Shc1 as a critical intermediate linking Syk phosphorylation to NOX2 activation. Shc1 phosphorylation during phagocytosis is dependent on Src family kinases and Syk, while genetic ablation of SHC1 reduced superoxide production by 40%. Proximity ligation assays reveal enhanced Shc1-p47phox interactions in PTP1B-deficient cells during phagocytosis. These findings establish an SFK-Syk-Shc1-NOX2 signaling axis that PTP1B negatively regulates at MCS between the ER and the forming phagosome, providing new mechanistic insights into antimicrobial responses during phagocytosis.
