## 摘要
肠上皮紧密连接（TJ）屏障功能缺陷和内质网（ER）应激是炎症性肠病（IBD）的核心病理特征，但内质网应激导致 TJ 破坏的分子机制仍不清楚。本研究探讨了内质网应激期间自噬在调节肠道 TJ 稳态中的作用。在发炎的克罗恩病组织和慢性葡聚糖硫酸钠（DSS）结肠炎中，内质网应激水平升高。在人肠上皮 Caco-2 单层细胞、小鼠结肠和人结肠外植体中，用衣霉素、毒胡萝卜素或布雷菲德菌素 A 诱导内质网应激会破坏 TJ 屏障的完整性，表现为跨上皮电阻降低和旁细胞通透性增加。内质网应激选择性地增加了成孔 TJ 蛋白 claudin-2 并改变了 occludin 的定位，而没有显著影响其他 claudins。用雷帕霉素药理激活自噬可减轻内质网应激，恢复 TJ 屏障功能，减少 claudin-2 积累，并维持 occludin 定位。相反，CRISPR-Cas9 介导的自噬基因 ATG7 缺失在体外加剧了内质网应激、细胞凋亡和 TJ 屏障功能障碍，而肠上皮特异性 Atg7 敲除小鼠在体内表现出增强的内质网应激诱导的肠道通透性。从机制上讲，持续的内质网应激通过 IRE1 激酶信号传导损害自噬流，导致 p62 和 claudin-2 积累。抑制 IRE1 激酶活性可恢复自噬，降低 claudin-2 水平，并维持 TJ 屏障功能。我们进一步确定接头相关激酶 1（AAK1）是内质网应激期间 IRE1 信号传导的下游介质，伴随 AP2M1 磷酸化增加和 claudin-2 运输改变。仅 claudin-2 过表达就会诱导内质网应激和溶酶体损伤，提示存在一种放大上皮损伤的正反馈机制。最后，肠道给予雷帕霉素可减轻小鼠 DSS 结肠炎中的内质网应激并恢复自噬。总之，这些发现确定了 IRE1-AAK1-自噬轴是内质网应激期间肠道 TJ 屏障完整性的关键调节因子。

## Abstract
Defective intestinal epithelial tight junction (TJ) barrier function and endoplasmic reticulum (ER) stress are central pathological features of inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), yet the molecular mechanisms ER stress to TJ disruption remains poorly understood. Here, we investigated the role of autophagy in regulating intestinal TJ homeostasis during ER stress. ER stress was elevated in inflamed Crohn disease tissue and chronic dextran sulfate sodium (DSS) colitis. In human intestinal epithelial Caco-2 monolayers, murine colon, and human colonic explants, induction of ER stress with tunicamycin, thapsigargin, or brefeldin A disrupted TJ barrier integrity, as demonstrated by reduced transepithelial electrical resistance and increased paracellular permeability. ER stress selectively increased the pore-forming TJ protein claudin-2 and altered occludin localization without significantly affecting other claudins. Pharmacologic activation of autophagy with rapamycin attenuated ER stress, restored TJ barrier function, reduced claudin-2 accumulation, and preserved occludin localization. Conversely, CRISPR-Cas9-mediated deletion of autophagy gene ATG7 exacerbated ER stress, apoptosis, and TJ barrier dysfunction in vitro, while intestinal epithelial-specific Atg7 knockout mice exhibited enhanced ER stress-induced intestinal permeability in-vivo. Mechanistically, prolonged ER stress impaired autophagic flux through IRE1 kinase signaling, resulting in accumulation of p62 and claudin-2. Inhibition of IRE1 kinase activity restored autophagy, reduced claudin-2 levels, and preserved TJ barrier function. We further identified adaptor-associated kinase 1 (AAK1) as a downstream mediator of IRE1 signaling during ER stress, with increased AP2M1 phosphorylation and altered claudin-2 trafficking. Claudin-2 overexpression alone induced ER stress and lysosomal damage, suggesting a feed-forward mechanism amplifying epithelial injury. Finally, enteric rapamycin administration reduced ER stress and restored autophagy in murine DSS colitis. Collectively, these findings identify an IRE1-AAK1-autophagy axis as a critical regulator of intestinal TJ barrier integrity during ER stress.

#### 1. 核心问题与整体含义（研究动机和背景）
炎症性肠病（IBD）的两个关键病理特征是肠上皮紧密连接（TJ）屏障功能障碍（即“肠漏”）和内质网（ER）应激。虽然已知内质网应激会破坏肠道屏障，但其具体的分子机制，尤其是自噬在这一过程中的调节作用尚不明确。本研究旨在探究内质网应激如何通过影响自噬流来改变紧密连接蛋白（特别是成孔蛋白 claudin-2）的稳态，从而导致屏障崩溃。

#### 2. 提出的方法论
