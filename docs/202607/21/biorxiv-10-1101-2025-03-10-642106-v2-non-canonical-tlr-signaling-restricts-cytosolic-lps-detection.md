## 摘要
脂多糖（LPS）的细胞内感应是病原体检测的重要组成部分，支配着先天免疫反应。然而，如何控制这一过程以维持稳态并消退炎症尚不清楚。在这里，我们展示了 MARCO 是一种诱饵 LPS 传感器，对于抑制 caspase 11 活性和非经典炎症小体至关重要。值得注意的是，MARCO 的表达受一条非经典 TLR 信号通路控制，该通路涉及代谢物衣康酸（itaconate）、自噬接头蛋白 p62 和转录因子 NRF2。在干扰素（IFN）存在的情况下，非经典 TLR 信号传导受损，NRF2 依赖性基因表达终止，从而削弱了 MARCO 的表达并允许非经典炎症小体的最佳激活。MARCO 的缺失增强了非经典炎症小体的激活，并使小鼠对败血症休克敏感。总之，本研究确定 MARCO 是一种此前未知的 LPS 传感器，受非经典 TLR 信号通路调节，并揭示了一个复杂的稳态开关，该开关允许最佳的免疫反应和炎症消退。

## Abstract
Intracellular sensing of lipopolysaccharide (LPS) is an essential component of pathogen detection that governs the innate immune response. However, how this process is controlled to maintain homeostasis and resolve inflammation is unclear. Here, we show that MARCO is a decoy LPS sensor crucial for restraining caspase 11 activity and the non-canonical inflammasome. Remarkably, MARCO expression is controlled by a non-canonical TLR signaling pathway involving the metabolite itaconate, the autophagy adaptor protein p62, and the transcription factor NRF2. In the presence of IFN, non-canonical TLR signaling is impaired and NRF2 dependent gene expression is terminated. Thus, impairing MARCO expression and licensing optimal activation of the non-canonical inflammasome. Loss of MARCO augments non-canonical inflammasome activation and sensitizes mice to septic shock. Together, this study identifies MARCO as a previously unknown LPS sensor that is regulated by a non-canonical TLR signaling pathway and reveals an intricate homeostatic switch that allows for optimal immune responses and resolution of inflammation.
