## 摘要
血小板因子 4 (PF4) 是一种阳离子抗菌肽，作为髓系特异性吞噬受体 CR3 (Mac-1, CD11b/CD18) 的配体。我们此前证明，重组二聚体 PF4 (rdPF4) 具有细菌调理素的功能，可增强对革兰氏阳性金黄色葡萄球菌的吞噬作用，并促进感染性腹膜炎小鼠模型中抗生素敏感型和耐甲氧西林金黄色葡萄球菌的清除。在本研究中，我们通过评估 rdPF4 对革兰氏阴性有荚膜肺炎克雷伯菌（一种世卫组织细菌重点病原体）吞噬作用的影响，探讨了 rdPF4 是否具有病原体通用性。我们证明，rdPF4 增强了多种小鼠和人类巨噬细胞系以及原代中性粒细胞和巨噬细胞对活的和热灭活的高毒力 K2 型及低毒力 K3 型肺炎克雷伯菌株的 CR3 介导的吞噬作用。它还增加了对耐碳青霉烯类肺炎克雷伯菌的吞噬作用。rdPF4 并不直接杀灭细菌，而是作为调理素结合到带负电荷的细菌荚膜上，并在白细胞上产生 CR3 的识别位点。在小鼠败血症模型中，单剂量 rdPF4 显著增强了肺部、肝脏和腹膜的细菌清除能力，并减轻了菌血症。组织学分析显示，rdPF4 为肺和肝组织提供了实质性保护，使其免受肺炎克雷伯菌诱导的损伤。与这些发现一致，rdPF4 治疗提高了受感染小鼠的存活率。这些结果表明，rdPF4 有效地靶向了肺炎克雷伯菌的关键毒力因子——荚膜，从而降低了细菌逃避宿主免疫反应的能力。总之，数据表明存在一种共同机制，即阳离子 rdPF4 通过结合革兰氏阴性和革兰氏阳性细菌的负电荷表面，削弱了它们的抗吞噬特性。

## Abstract
Platelet Factor 4 (PF4), a cationic antimicrobial peptide, serves as a ligand for the myeloid-specific phagocytic receptor CR3 (Mac-1, CD11b/CD18). We previously demonstrated that recombinant dimeric PF4 (rdPF4) functions as a bacterial opsonin, enhancing phagocytosis of Gram-positive Staphylococcus aureus and facilitating clearance of both antibiotic-susceptible and methicillin-resistant S. aureus in a mouse model of infectious peritonitis. In this study, we examined whether rdPF4 is pathogen-agnostic by assessing its effect on phagocytosis of Gram-negative encapsulated Klebsiella pneumoniae, a WHO Bacterial Priority Pathogen. We demonstrate that rdPF4 enhances CR3-mediated phagocytosis of both live and heat-inactivated high-virulence K2 and low-virulence K3 strains of K. pneumoniae by various mouse and human macrophage cell lines, as well as primary neutrophils and macrophages. It also increased phagocytosis of carbapenem-resistant K. pneumoniae. rdPF4 did not directly kill bacteria but acted as an opsonin binding to the negatively charged bacterial capsule and creating recognition sites for CR3 on leukocytes. In a mouse sepsis model, a single dose of rdPF4 significantly enhanced bacterial clearance from the lungs, liver, and peritoneum and reduced bacteremia. Histological analyses showed that rdPF4 provided substantial protection to lung and liver tissues against K. pneumoniae-induced damage. Consistent with these findings, rdPF4 treatment increased the survival rates of infected mice. These results show that rdPF4 effectively targets the capsule, a key virulence factor of K. pneumoniae, thereby reducing the bacteriums ability to evade the host immune response. Overall, the data suggest a common mechanism in which cationic rdPF4, by binding to the negatively charged surfaces of both Gram-negative and Gram-positive bacteria, diminishes their antiphagocytic properties.
