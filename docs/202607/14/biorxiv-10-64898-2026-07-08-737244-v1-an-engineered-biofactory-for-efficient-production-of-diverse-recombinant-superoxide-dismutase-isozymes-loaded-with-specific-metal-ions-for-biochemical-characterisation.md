## 摘要
背景：对普遍存在的铁或锰依赖性超氧化物歧化酶（SodFMs）家族同工酶进行生化、生物物理和结构表征，需要纯化高质量的重组酶制剂。确定其关键生化参数（即催化金属偏好性）需要对比仅负载铁的样品与仅负载锰的样品的催化周转率。然而，通过大肠杆菌（Escherichia coli）细胞内异源过表达制备的 SodFMs 重组制剂，极易受到宿主内源性 SodFMs 的污染，即使是极低水平的污染也会产生干扰，因为大肠杆菌的锰超氧化物歧化酶（MnSOD）和铁超氧化物歧化酶（FeSOD）在负载相应金属时均表现出极高的周转率。为解决这一问题，我们构建了一株缺失内源性 SodFMs 的大肠杆菌菌株。本文中，我们对该大肠杆菌 BL21 (DE3) ΔsodAΔsodB 菌株进行了表征，确定了 SodFM 缺失的生理影响，并证明了其在生产用于体外表征和应用的重组 SodFMs 方面的实用性。结果：基因组分析证实了目标基因的精准缺失，且无脱靶效应。生长、表达、元素分析和蛋白质组学数据证实，该菌株除已知的无法在葡萄糖上生长（可通过异源表达 SodFM 克服）外，无其他生理缺陷。我们展示了该菌株在高效生产多种重组 SodFMs（包括高度分化且研究较少的同工酶）方面的用途，并证明了其能够精确控制异源表达蛋白的金属负载。结论：本文描述的大肠杆菌菌株是生产重组 SodFMs 的有用微生物细胞工厂，应作为首选表达宿主得到广泛应用，从而为研究这一卓越金属酶家族的生化、生物物理和结构特性提供更高效的蛋白质生产手段。

## Abstract
BackgroundBiochemical, biophysical and structural characterisation of isozymes from the ubiquitous family of iron- or manganese-dependent superoxide dismutases (SodFMs) requires the purification of high-quality preparations of recombinant enzymes. Determination of their key biochemical parameter, their catalytic metal-preference, requires the comparison of the catalytic turnover of samples loaded exclusively with iron versus samples loaded exclusively with manganese. Both of these aims are inhibited by the potential contamination of recombinant preparations of SodFMs, prepared by heterologous overexpression inside Escherichia coli cells, by even low levels of endogenous SodFMs from the host, both of which show very high turnover with either manganese (E. coli MnSOD) or iron (FeSOD). To overcome this problem, we created a strain of E. coli lacking the endogenous SodFMs. Here, we characterised this E. coli BL21 (DE3) {Delta}sodA{Delta}sodB strain, determining the physiological effects of SodFM deletion and demonstrating its utility for producing recombinant SodFMs for in vitro characterisation and use.

ResultsGenomic analysis verified the targeted gene deletions, without off-target effects. Growth, expression, elemental analysis, and proteomic data confirmed a lack of physiological defects of the strain except for a known inability to grow on glucose, which is overcome by heterologous SodFM expression. We demonstrate the utility of the strain for the efficient production of diverse recombinant SodFMs, including highly divergent, understudied isozymes, including the ability to precisely control the metal-loading of the heterologously expressed protein.

ConclusionsThe E. coli strain described herein is a useful microbial cell factory for production of recombinant SodFMs, which should find widespread utility as expression host of choice, enabling more efficient production of protein for studies of the biochemical, biophysical and structural properties of this remarkable family of metalloenzymes.
