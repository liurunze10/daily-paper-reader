Abstract 28 

Tumor -associated macrophages (TAMs) are abundant in intrahepatic 29 

cholangiocarcinoma (ICC)  and  correlate with poor prognosis, but their 30 

non -immunosuppressive  roles remain unclear.  This study aimed  to define TAM 31 

transcriptional heterogeneity and map their interactions with ICC cells that promote 32 

malignancy beyond immune suppression.  Single -cell  and spatial transcriptome 33 

analysis with mouse and human ICC tissues revealed a TAM  subpopulation ,34 

designated  Mφ_ VEGFA , that  emerged as the major macrophage population  from the 35 

intermediate  stage of ICC  development onward.  Data from  Gene Expression Omnibus 36 

(GEO) and spatial transcriptome indicate that  Mφ_ VEGFA  was associated with  poor 37 

prognosis and spatially localized to the ICC cells.  Cell co -culture and c ell -cell 38 

communication analyses  revealed  that  Mφ_ VEGFA  promoted  ICC cell proliferation 39 

via  secreted OSM and downstream O SMR –STAT3 –CCND1  axis , and  enhanced  ICC 40 

cell  invasion through THBS1  and downstream  CD47 –mTOR /DBN1  signaling .41 

Critically,  multiplex immunofluorescence with  human  ICC tissue microarray 42 

indicated that  OSM  and  THBS1  were co -elevated in  human  ICC s, and  dual blockade 43 

of both targets inhibit ed  ICC progression  in mouse models.  This study uncovers the 44 

direct and critical roles of  Mφ_ VEGFA  in  driving  ICC progression with paracrine 45 

signaling  and  further  highlight s the potential of  targeting  macrophages  in ICC 46 

treatment .47

Introduction 50 

The incidence of intrahepatic cholangiocarcinoma (ICC), which arises from the 51 

intrahepatic bile duct epithelium, is experiencing a global rise. Notably, its subtle 52 

early symptoms, aggressive nature, and chemotherapy resistance contribute to a 53 

5-year survival rate of below 10%  (1) . Targeted therapies and immune checkpoint 54 

inhibitors provide benefits in some solid tumors; nevertheless, responses in ICC 55 

remain inconsistent owing to its complex tumor microenvironment (TME)  (2) .56 

Tumor -associated macrophages (TAMs)  are  the most abundant immune cells in the 57 

ICC TME,  and  promote immune suppression, angiogenesis , stromal remodeling,  and 58 

subsequent  progression and metastasis  (2, 3) . Current TAM -targeting strategies, 59 

including depletion, reprogramming, recruitment control, and functional regulation, 60 

exhibit variable efficacy  (4, 5) . Thus, dissecting communication cascades between 61 

ICC cells and TAMs  may help  identify  novel  therapeutic targets  for  ICC  therapy .62 

63 

TAMs are highly heterogenous and d istinct TAM  subpopulation s are  reportedly 64 

associated with ICC progression. MARCO⁺ TAMs correlate with 65 

epithelial -mesenchymal transition (EMT), angiogenesis, and hypoxia  in the ICC 66 

progression  (6) . S100P  and  SPP1 -markers, distinguishing perihilar and peripheral 67 

cholangiocarcinoma, exhibit differential macrophage infiltration tied to prognosis and 68 

grade  (7) . Furthermore, spatial distribution influences TAM functions  (8) , and 69 

spatial -omics studies  further revealed  TAM heterogeneity  (9, 10) . Specifically, TAMs 70 

at invasive fronts mainly express pro -angiogenic factors such as VEGFA, whereas 71

To characterize macrophage subsets involved in ICC malignancy, we established an 88

Histological examination of mouse ICC tissues collected at four time points revealed 90

Discussion 347 

TAMs in ICC have been associated with poor prognosis through tumor growth and 348 

invasiveness promotion (25, 26) . Recent single -cell omics research has revealed the 349 

molecular diversity and functions of TAMs in cancer  (27) . Nonetheless,  the specific 350 

subgroups driving ICC malignancy and its interactions with tumor cells remain 351 

unclear. Using a time -resolved ICC mouse model with integrated multi -omics, we 352 

identified Mφ_ VEGFA as a poor -prognosis TAM subset in ICC. Notably, the 353 

prognostic significance of Mφ_ VEGFA was also observed in hepatocellular 354 

carcinoma  (13) , underscoring its prevalence across liver cancers  (28) .355 

356 

Moreover, our study uncovers the mechanism underlying  Mφ_ VEGFA  to promote 357 

ICC progression or metastasis instead of suppressing the tumor immune 358 

microenvironment. Specifically, M φ_ VEGFA promotes ICC cell proliferation through 359 

OSM -OSMR and downstream p -STAT3 and CCND1, corroborating  the findings that 360 

OSM promotes cancer cell proliferation in  glioblastoma  (29)  and pancreatic ductal 361 

adenocarcinoma  (30) . Conversely,  Mφ_VEGFA exerts  its pro -invasive role through 362 

secreted THBS1 to activate CD47 signaling in ICC cells. Notably,  THBS1 has been 363 

reported to be released from tumor -infiltrating monocyte -like cells and to induce an 364 

immunosuppressive TME to boost metastasis  (31) . Conversely, we demonstrate that 365 

CD47  activates mTOR and DBN1 in ICC cells but not IL6/STAT3, causing EMT and 366 

cell skeleton remodeling  (32) .367 

368

Finally,  our findings offer a new therapeutic strategy for ICC treatment by targeting 369 

molecules derived from M φ_ VEGFA, which  secrete  OSM and THBS1 to accelerate 370 

ICC growth and metastasis via OSMR and CD47, respectively.  Combined blockades 371 

of OSM –OSMR and THBS1 –CD47 signaling retarded growth and restrained 372 

metastasis in primary ICC mouse models, while single inhibition generated modest 373 

efficacy. Consistently, a clinical trial demonstrated that targeting OSM -OSMR 374 

signaling alone led to poor outcomes or drug resistance  (33) . Additionally, OSM and 375 

THBS1 were concurrently upregulated in 13 –29% of patients with ICC . Thus, 376 

targeting dual modulators of Mφ_ VEGFA provides a potentially more robust 377 

therapeutic option for those patients with ICC. 378 

379 

Nonetheless, this  study also has some limitations. First, the role of  Mφ_ VEGFA  in 380 

promoting ICC migration and invasion was not fully illustrated using  matched early 381 

and late -stage  clinical  samples.  As ICC is usually diagnosed at advanced stages and 382 

progresses rapidly, it is difficult to obtain paired pre - and post -metastatic ICC tissues 383 

from the same patients. Therefore, it is challenging for us to determine the prevalence 384 

of M φ_ VEGFA  in metastatic ICC. Second, we were unable to explore the possibility 385 

of specific M φ_ VEGFA  subpopulation depletion to confirm its unique role in favoring 386 

ICC progression. Clearly, this caveat is caused by a technical limitation, as no reliable 387 

technique is available to delete a specific macrophage subpopulation to date. Finally, 388 

the therapeutic value of targeting  OSM  or THBS1 was not fully demonstrated in this 389 

study. In this regard, organoid or patient -derived xenograft mouse models are more 390
