## 摘要
引言：肠道屏障功能衰竭是溃疡性结肠炎（UC）和克罗恩病（CD）这两种炎症性肠病（IBD）的关键特征。肠道屏障（或称肠黏膜屏障）由黏液和紧密互连的单层肠上皮细胞（IEC）组成。这些成分共同作用，将肠道微生物群限制在肠腔内；当其失效时，微生物、微生物产物或微生物成分会引起宿主组织的损伤、炎症和免疫激活。我们此前曾报道，结肠黏液中的高迁移率族蛋白 B1（HMGB1）能聚集细菌，限制细菌穿透黏液，并防止细菌粘附到宿主组织。在活动期 UC 病变中，上皮表面相关的 HMGB1 减少，且低水平的 HMGB1 与表达携带 HMGB1 分子靶点（ToH1）的粘附素的高水平组织粘附细菌相关。本研究旨在确定活动期 CD 患者病变中的 HMGB1 防御是否也受到损害。方法：采用免疫荧光显微镜观察 CD 患者和非 IBD 对照组患者结肠切除组织中的黏液和 HMGB1。结果：活动期 CD 病变区域存在 IEC 缺失或从底层组织脱离的现象，同时伴有黏液厚度增加以及充满 α-连接岩藻糖残基阳性黏液的杯状细胞。活动期 CD 病变中表面相关的 HMGB1 同样有所减少。结论：与对照组患者相比，CD 患者组织表现出细胞和非细胞性肠道屏障缺陷。我们观察到了此前报道的 CD 病变中 IEC 屏障完整性丧失以及黏液数量和分布的异常。我们还首次报道了 CD 与结肠表面 HMGB1 防御降低有关。

## Abstract
IntroductionIntestinal barrier failure is a key characteristic of both kinds of inflammatory bowel disease (IBD), ulcerative colitis (UC) and Crohns disease (CD). The intestinal barrier, or gut mucosal barrier, is composed of mucus and a tightly interconnected single layer of intestinal epithelial cells (IEC) that line the gut. Together, these components work to contain the gut microbiota within the gut lumen and when they fail, microbes, microbial products, or microbial components cause damage, inflammation, and immune activation in host tissue. We previously reported that High mobility group box 1 (HMGB1) in colonic mucus aggregates bacteria, limits bacterial invasion through mucus, and prevents bacteria from adhering to host tissue. Epithelial surface-associated HMGB1 is decreased in active UC lesions and low levels of HMGB1 are associated with high levels of tissue-adherent bacteria expressing adhesins carrying the molecular target of HMGB1 (ToH1). The study reported here was designed to determine whether HMGB1 defense is also compromised in active lesions from CD patients.

MethodsImmunofluorescence microscopy was used to visualize mucus and HMGB1 in tissue from colonic resections performed in CD and non-IBD control patients.

ResultsActive CD lesions had areas where the IEC were absent or pulling away from underlying tissue along with areas of increased mucus thickness and goblet cells full of mucus highly positive for alpha-linked-fucose residues. The surface associated HMGB1 was also decreased in active CD lesions.

ConclusionTissue from CD patients exhibited cellular and acellular intestinal barrier defects in comparison to control patients. We observed the previously reported loss of IEC barrier integrity and abnormalities in the amount and distribution of mucus in CD lesions. We also report for the first time that CD is associated with decreased HMGB1 defense at the colon surface.
