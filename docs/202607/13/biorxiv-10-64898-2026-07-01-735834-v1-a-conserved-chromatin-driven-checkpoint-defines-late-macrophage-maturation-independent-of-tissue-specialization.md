## 摘要
巨噬细胞的身份通常被视为个体发育和组织特异性印记的产物。在这里，我们发现了一个保守的、由染色质驱动的成熟检查点，该检查点独立于初始谱系定型，并广泛存在于各种组织背景中。通过使用长期骨髓来源的巨噬细胞培养物，我们发现了一个以协调的转录和表观基因组重塑为特征的晚期转变。通过与体内发育、组织驻留和单核细胞重群体数据集的整合，证明了该程序在不同发育起源、组织、物种和实验系统中都是保守的，揭示了一个此前未被认识的巨噬细胞成熟阶段。在功能上，晚期成熟在保留核心巨噬细胞活动的同时，促进了溶酶体扩张并从根本上重构了先天免疫反应性。成熟的巨噬细胞对干扰素和微生物危险信号表现出增强的刺激特异性反应，同时伴随着代谢和炎症能力的提高，并限制了干扰素诱导的转录记忆。总之，我们的研究结果将巨噬细胞晚期成熟确定为一个保守的调节检查点，通过塑造先天免疫功能的染色质重塑，重新编程了先天免疫反应的逻辑。

## Abstract
Macrophage identity is widely viewed as a product of ontogeny and tissue-specific imprinting. Here, we identify a conserved, chromatin-driven maturation checkpoint that operates independently of initial lineage commitment and broadly across tissue contexts. Using long-term bone marrow-derived macrophage cultures, we uncover a late-stage transition characterized by coordinated transcriptional and epigenomic remodeling. Integration with in vivo developmental, tissue-resident, and monocyte-repopulation datasets demonstrates that this program is conserved across ontogenies, tissues, species, and experimental systems, revealing a previously unrecognized stage of macrophage maturation. Functionally, late maturation preserves core macrophage activities while promoting lysosomal expansion and fundamentally rewiring innate immune responsiveness. Mature macrophages display enhanced stimulus-specific responses to interferons and microbial danger signals, coupled to increased metabolic and inflammatory competence while restricting interferon-induced transcriptional memory. Together, our findings identify late macrophage maturation as a conserved regulatory checkpoint that reprograms the logic of innate immune responsiveness through chromatin remodeling shaping innate immune function.
