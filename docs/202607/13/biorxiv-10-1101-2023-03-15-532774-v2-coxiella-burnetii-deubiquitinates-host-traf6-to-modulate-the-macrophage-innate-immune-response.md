## 摘要
贝氏柯克斯体（Coxiella burnetii）是一种具有高度传染性、通过气溶胶传播的专性胞内寄生菌，可引起人类Q热，并在肺部感染期间在肺泡巨噬细胞内复制。成功的感染需要贝氏柯克斯体IVB型分泌系统（T4BSS），该系统将细菌效应蛋白递送到宿主细胞质中，以重塑宿主生理过程并抑制先天免疫反应。在肺部，IL-17通过激活ACT1-TRAF6依赖性通路促进抗菌免疫，从而驱动炎症基因表达、活性氧产生和中性粒细胞招募。然而，胞内病原体如何破坏IL-17信号通路以促进其在巨噬细胞内的存活仍不清楚。本研究表明，贝氏柯克斯体通过靶向TRAF6（一种先天免疫信号传导的核心泛素依赖性调节因子）来逃避IL-17介导的宿主防御。通过ACT1-TRAF6进行的IL-17信号传导限制了贝氏柯克斯体在巨噬细胞中的活力；然而，贝氏柯克斯体削弱了IL-17诱导的NF-κB和MAPK激活，特别是JNK磷酸化。贝氏柯克斯体的T4BSS活性抑制了IL-17驱动的活性氧产生和中性粒细胞招募。我们鉴定出贝氏柯克斯体T4BSS效应蛋白EmcB是一种靶向TRAF6的去泛素酶，它能破坏IL-17依赖性的抗菌和趋化反应。这些发现共同揭示了贝氏柯克斯体通过靶向TRAF6下调胞内先天免疫信号传导，从而逃避巨噬细胞的抗菌反应并限制中性粒细胞介导的免疫。意义声明：贝氏柯克斯体引起Q热并在巨噬细胞（通常有助于清除吸入病原体的免疫细胞）内存活。本研究确定了贝氏柯克斯体削弱巨噬细胞防御的一种机制。我们表明该细菌破坏了巨噬细胞的IL-17信号传导，这是一种促进炎症基因表达、活性氧产生和中性粒细胞招募的免疫通路。从机制上讲，贝氏柯克斯体利用分泌的酶EmcB靶向TRAF6，而TRAF6是IL-17依赖性抗菌反应所需的关键信号蛋白。这些发现揭示了胞内细菌病原体如何使关键免疫信号枢纽失活以促进存活并限制先天免疫激活，为病原体如何操纵宿主免疫提供了更广泛的见解。

## Abstract
Coxiella burnetii is a highly infectious, aerosol-transmitted obligate intracellular bacterium that causes human Q fever and replicates within alveolar macrophages during pulmonary infection. Successful infection requires the C. burnetii Type IVB secretion system (T4BSS), which delivers bacterial effector proteins into the host cytoplasm to remodel host processes and suppress the innate immune response. In the lung, IL-17 promotes antibacterial immunity by activating ACT1-TRAF6-dependent pathways that drive inflammatory gene expression, reactive oxygen species production, and neutrophil recruitment. However, how intracellular pathogens subvert IL-17 signaling pathways to promote survival within macrophages remains poorly understood. Here, we show that C. burnetii evades IL-17-mediated host defense by targeting TRAF6, a central ubiquitin-dependent regulator of innate immune signaling. IL-17 signaling through ACT1-TRAF6 restricts C. burnetii viability in macrophages; however, C. burnetii impairs IL-17-induced NF-{kappa}B and MAPK activation, particularly JNK phosphorylation. C. burnetii T4BSS activity suppresses IL-17-driven reactive oxygen species production and neutrophil recruitment. We identify the C. burnetii T4BSS effector EmcB as a TRAF6-targeting deubiquitinase that disrupts IL-17-dependent antimicrobial and chemotactic responses. Together these findings reveal that C. burnetii downregulates intracellular innate immune signaling by targeting TRAF6, enabling evasion of the macrophage antimicrobial response and limiting neutrophil-mediated immunity.

Significance StatementCoxiella burnetii causes Q fever and survives inside macrophages, immune cells that normally help eliminate inhaled pathogens. This study identifies a mechanism by which C. burnetii weakens macrophage defenses. We show that the bacterium disrupts macrophage IL-17 signaling, an immune pathway that promotes inflammatory gene expression, reactive oxygen species production, and neutrophil recruitment. Mechanistically, C. burnetii uses the secreted enzyme EmcB to target TRAF6, a central signaling protein required for IL-17-dependent antimicrobial responses. These findings reveal how an intracellular bacterial pathogen disables a key immune signaling hub to promote survival and limit innate immune activation, providing broader insight into how pathogens manipulate host immunity.
