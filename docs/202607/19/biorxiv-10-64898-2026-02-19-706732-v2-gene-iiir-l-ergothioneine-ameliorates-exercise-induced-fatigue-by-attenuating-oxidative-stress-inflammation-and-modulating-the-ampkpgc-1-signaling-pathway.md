## 摘要
背景：运动性疲劳是一种涉及氧化应激、炎症和代谢紊劳的复杂生理现象。麦角硫因 (EGT) 是一种具有强效抗氧化特性的天然氨基酸，其潜在的健康益处已引起广泛关注。本研究旨在评估 Gene III(R) EGT 在力竭运动小鼠模型中的抗疲劳效果，并阐明其潜在机制。方法：将雄性 C57BL/6 小鼠随机分为五组：对照组 (CTL)、低剂量 EGT 组 (EGT-L, 10 mg/kg)、中剂量 EGT 组 (EGT-M, 30 mg/kg)、高剂量 EGT 组 (EGT-H, 50 mg/kg) 和阳性对照组 (辅酶 Q10, 50 mg/kg)。小鼠接受为期 4 周的跑步机训练方案，随后进行力竭跑步测试。我们测量了运动表现，并在多个时间点采集血液和骨骼肌样本，以评估生化指标、炎症细胞因子、抗氧化状态和关键信号蛋白。结果：补充 Gene III(R) EGT（尤其是中、高剂量）显著延长了力竭时间和跑步距离。与对照组相比，EGT 治疗显著降低了运动后的乳酸 (LA)、乳酸脱氢酶 (LDH) 和血尿素氮 (BUN) 水平。此外，Gene III(R) EGT 抑制了运动诱导的促炎细胞因子（包括 IL-1β、IL-6 和 TNF-α）的升高。EGT 的抗疲劳作用还与丙二醛 (MDA) 的减少以及超氧化物歧化酶 (SOD) 和谷胱甘肽过氧化物酶 (GSH-Px) 活性的增加有关。机制上，EGT 促进了骨骼肌中 AMP 活化蛋白激酶 (AMPK) 的磷酸化和过氧化物酶体增殖物激活受体 γ 共激活因子-1α (PGC-1α) 的表达，同时增加了 Bcl-2/Bax 比值，表明其增强了线粒体生物合成并减少了细胞凋亡。结论：我们的研究结果表明，Gene III(R) EGT 能有效增强运动表现并缓解疲劳。其潜在机制涉及减轻氧化应激和炎症，以及激活 AMPK/PGC-1α 信号通路以促进线粒体功能和细胞保护。这些结果突显了 Gene III(R) EGT 作为对抗运动性疲劳营养补充剂的潜力。关键词：麦角硫因；运动性疲劳；氧化应激；AMPK；PGC-1α；线粒体生物合成

## Abstract
Background: Exercise-induced fatigue is a complex physiological phenomenon involving oxidative stress, inflammation, and metabolic disturbances. Ergothioneine (EGT), a naturally occurring amino acid with potent antioxidant properties, has garnered interest for its potential health benefits. This study aimed to evaluate the anti-fatigue effects of Gene III(R) EGT in a mouse model of exhaustive exercise and to elucidate its underlying mechanisms. Methods: Male C57BL/6 mice were randomly divided into five groups: a control group (CTL), low-dose EGT (EGT-L, 10 mg/kg), medium-dose EGT (EGT-M, 30 mg/kg), high-dose EGT (EGT-H, 50 mg/kg), and a positive control group (Coenzyme Q10, 50 mg/kg). Mice were subjected to a 4-week treadmill training protocol, followed by an exhaustive running test. We measured exercise performance and collected blood and skeletal muscle samples at multiple time points to assess biochemical markers, inflammatory cytokines, antioxidant status, and key signaling proteins. Results: Gene III(R) EGT supplementation, particularly at medium and high doses, significantly extended the time to exhaustion and running distance. Compared to the control group, EGT treatment significantly reduced post-exercise levels of lactic acid (LA), lactate dehydrogenase (LDH), and blood urea nitrogen (BUN). Furthermore, Gene III(R) EGT suppressed the exercise-induced increase in pro-inflammatory cytokines, including IL-1{beta}, IL-6, and TNF-. The anti-fatigue effect of EGT was also associated with a reduction in malondialdehyde (MDA) and an increase in the activities of superoxide dismutase (SOD) and glutathione peroxidase (GSH-Px). Mechanistically, EGT promoted the phosphorylation of AMP-activated protein kinase (AMPK) and the expression of peroxisome proliferator-activated receptor-gamma coactivator-1 alpha (PGC-1) in skeletal muscle, while also increasing the Bcl-2/Bax ratio, suggesting enhanced mitochondrial biogenesis and reduced apoptosis. Conclusions: Our findings demonstrate that Gene III(R) EGT effectively enhances exercise performance and alleviates fatigue. The underlying mechanisms involve the mitigation of oxidative stress and inflammation, as well as the activation of the AMPK/PGC-1 signaling pathway to promote mitochondrial function and cellular protection. These results highlight the potential of Gene III(R) EGT as a nutritional supplement for combating exercise-induced fatigue. Keywords: Ergothioneine; Exercise-induced fatigue; Oxidative stress; AMPK; PGC-1; Mitochondrial biogenesis
