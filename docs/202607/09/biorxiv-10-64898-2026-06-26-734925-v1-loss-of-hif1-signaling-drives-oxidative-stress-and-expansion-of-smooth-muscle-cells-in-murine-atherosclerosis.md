## 摘要
背景：随着动脉粥样硬化病变的增长，病变内部会出现缺氧，诱导缺氧诱导因子-1（HIF1）的核转位及代谢重编程。虽然其在斑块巨噬细胞和内皮细胞中的作用已有研究，但缺氧的斑块内部主要由平滑肌细胞（SMC）衍生的细胞占据，而缺氧信号在这些细胞中的作用尚不明确。本研究旨在探讨SMC谱系细胞中Hif1a的缺失如何影响小鼠动脉粥样硬化的斑块进展和细胞表型。方法：在具有诱导型SMC特异性Hif1a缺失（Hif1aSMC-KO）及SMC衍生斑块细胞谱系追踪的小鼠中诱导动脉粥样硬化。通过主动脉根部切片定量分析斑块大小、坏死核心大小、钙化情况以及SMC衍生细胞的表型，并通过单细胞RNA测序（scRNA-seq）绘制基因表达变化图谱。同时，在体外培养的SMC系中利用siRNA介导Hif1a敲低，并将其暴露于缺氧环境下，以评估线粒体功能和活性氧（ROS）的产生。结果：与同窝对照组相比，Hif1aSMC-KO小鼠的斑块更大，坏死核心扩张且钙化增加。SMC衍生斑块细胞数量更多，其中Col2a1+软骨样肌细胞比例更高，且增殖和凋亡标志物水平升高，而巨噬细胞和内皮细胞数量未受影响。单细胞RNA测序分析显示，线粒体基因（包括电子传递链转录本）发生严重失调，同时蛋白质折叠、蛋白酶体和氧化应激反应通路发生上调。在缺氧条件下的培养SMC中，Hif1a沉默增加了细胞数量，加剧了线粒体质子漏，并导致去极化、产生ROS的线粒体积累。对Hif1aSMC-KO小鼠斑块中SMC衍生细胞的进一步分析通过8OHdG染色证实了氧化应激的增加。结论：在小鼠动脉粥样硬化中，HIF1维持了SMC衍生斑块细胞的线粒体功能并抑制了氧化应激。其长期缺失会破坏氧化还原稳态并促进SMC的适应不良反应，从而导致SMC驱动的斑块扩张、坏死和钙化。

## Abstract
BackgroundHypoxia develops within growing atherosclerotic lesions, inducing nuclear translocation of hypoxia-inducible factor-1 (HIF1) and metabolic reprogramming. Its role in plaque macrophages and endothelial cells has been studied, but the hypoxic plaque interior is dominated by smooth muscle cell (SMC)-derived cells, for which the role of hypoxia signaling remains unclear. Here, we investigated how loss of Hif1a in SMC lineage cells impacts plaque progression and cell phenotype in murine atherosclerosis.

MethodsAtherosclerosis was induced in mice with inducible SMC-specific deletion of Hif1a (Hif1aSMC-KO) and lineage tracing of SMC-derived plaque cells. Plaque size, necrotic core size, calcification, and SMC-derived cell phenotypes were quantified in aortic root sections and gene expression changes mapped by single-cell RNA sequencing. In parallel, a cultured SMC line with or without siRNA-mediated Hif1a knockdown was exposed to hypoxia for assessments of mitochondrial function and reactive oxygen species production.

ResultsHif1aSMC-KO mice developed larger plaques, with expanded necrotic cores and increased calcification, compared with littermate controls. SMC-derived plaque cells were more abundant with a higher fraction of Col2a1+ chondromyocytes, and showed elevated markers of proliferation and apoptosis, whereas macrophage and endothelial cell numbers were unaffected. Single-cell RNA sequencing analysis revealed strong dysregulation of mitochondrial genes, including electron transport chain transcripts, along with upregulation of protein folding, proteasome, and oxidative stress response pathways. In cultured SMCs subjected to hypoxia, Hif1a silencing increased cell counts, aggravated mitochondrial proton leak, and led to the accumulation of depolarized, reactive oxygen species-generating mitochondria. Further analysis of SMC-derived cells in plaques from Hif1aSMC-KO mice confirmed increased oxidative stress by 8OHdG staining.

ConclusionsHIF1 maintains mitochondrial function and restrains oxidative stress in SMC-derived plaque cells in murine atherosclerosis. Its chronic loss destabilizes redox homeostasis and promotes maladaptive SMC responses, leading to SMC-driven plaque expansion, necrosis, and calcification.
