## 摘要
2型糖尿病与代谢压力和衰老驱动的全身性炎症密切相关。尽管与这些因素相关的胰腺炎症已有详尽记录，但免疫细胞群的动态及其分子变化仍不清楚。我们利用成像质谱流式术（IMC）和单细胞RNA测序（scRNA-seq），表征了西方饮食（WD）喂养和衰老过程中胰腺及胰岛内免疫细胞的变化。通过空间和转录组分析，我们定义了代谢和年龄相关压力条件下免疫细胞亚型的组成、激活状态以及推断的细胞间通讯程序。分析发现，在WD喂养和衰老过程中，整个胰腺内F4/80low巨噬细胞亚型和激活的效应样CD8+ T细胞均发生扩张。在胰岛内部，单细胞RNA测序鉴定出一群在过度营养期间扩张的、具有低F4/80表达的I型干扰素反应性巨噬细胞。值得注意的是，这些压力源诱发的I型干扰素反应存在差异：衰老与更经典的I型干扰素反应相关，而过度营养则诱导了更广泛的反应，包括STAT3相关的转录程序。我们进一步提供了在过度营养和衰老下，巨噬细胞与CD8+细胞毒性T细胞群之间细胞因子介导的通讯增强的证据。这些发现表明，代谢压力和衰老通过重叠但不同的免疫机制重塑胰腺炎症，涉及F4/80low巨噬细胞的扩张、不同I型干扰素程序的激活以及巨噬细胞与CD8+ T细胞通讯的增强。总之，这些发现提示，针对代谢压力与衰老驱动的2型糖尿病，可能需要不同的治疗方法来保护胰岛功能。文章亮点：1. 代谢压力和衰老通过重叠但不同的免疫机制重塑胰腺炎症。2. 空间和单细胞分析确定了代谢压力和衰老期间胰腺巨噬细胞群的保守重塑，并伴有细胞毒性T细胞反应的激活。3. 在胰岛中，巨噬细胞表现出不同的I型干扰素相关转录程序，衰老显示出更经典的干扰素反应，而代谢压力则诱发了包括STAT3相关转录特征在内的更广泛的炎症程序。4. 这些发现为理解代谢压力和衰老如何差异化地塑造胰腺炎症提供了一个框架。

## Abstract
Type 2 diabetes is linked to systemic inflammation driven by metabolic stress and aging. Although pancreatic inflammation associated with these factors is well documented, the dynamics of immune cell populations and their molecular changes remain poorly understood. We characterized immune cell alterations in the pancreas and pancreatic islets during Western diet (WD) feeding and aging using imaging mass cytometry (IMC) and single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq). Spatial and transcriptional analyses were performed to define immune cell subtype composition, activation states, and inferred cell-cell communication programs under metabolic and age-related stress conditions. Our analyses identified expansion of an F4/80low macrophage subtype and activated effector-like CD8+ T cells throughout the pancreas during WD feeding and aging. Within pancreatic islets, single-cell RNA sequencing identified a type I interferon-responsive macrophage population with low F4/80 expression that expanded during overnutrition. Notably, the type I interferon responses elicited by these stressors diverged: aging was associated with a more canonical type I interferon response, whereas overnutrition induced a broader response that included STAT3-associated transcriptional programs. We further provide evidence for enhanced cytokine-mediated communication between macrophages and a CD8+ cytotoxic T-cell population under overnutrition and aging. These findings show that metabolic stress and aging remodel pancreatic inflammation through overlapping but distinct immune mechanisms, involving expansion of F4/80low macrophages, activation of divergent type I interferon programs, and enhanced macrophage-CD8+ T-cell communication. Together, these findings suggest that distinct therapeutic approaches may be required to preserve islet function in type 2 diabetes driven by metabolic stress versus aging.
