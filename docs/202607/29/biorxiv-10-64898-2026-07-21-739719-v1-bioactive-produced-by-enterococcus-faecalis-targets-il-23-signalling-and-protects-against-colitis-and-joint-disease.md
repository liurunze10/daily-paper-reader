## 摘要
IL-23/Th17 轴是肠道和脊柱关节炎炎症的核心驱动因素，但将微生物信号与 IL-23 产生联系起来的上游调节机制尚未完全明确。NF-κB 信号传导，特别是通过 c-Rel 亚基，是 IL23A (p19) 的关键转录调节因子，使 c-Rel 成为黏膜炎症中的节点检查点。在此，我们证明源自粪肠球菌 (Enterococcus faecalis) AHG0090 的无细胞上清液 (AHG0090-CS) 可抑制 c-Rel 依赖性 IL-23 信号传导，并减轻肠道和关节疾病小鼠模型中的炎症病理。在 ZAP-70 突变 SKG 脊柱关节炎和回肠炎模型中，AHG0090-CS 显著减轻了柯德兰 (curdlan) 攻击后的体重减轻、关节评分和组织学肠道炎症。在 Winnie 自发性结肠炎模型中，治疗同样减少了炎症细胞因子的产生。从机制上讲，AHG0090-CS 降低了肠道组织和固有层髓系细胞中 IL-23p19 mRNA 和蛋白的表达，并伴有核内 c-Rel 强度的降低。抑制作用延伸至下游 IL-23 相关细胞因子，包括 IL-17A、GM-CSF、MCP-1 和 IL-6。在人类外周血单核细胞和巨噬细胞中，AHG0090-CS 减弱了 LPS 诱导的 IL-23 和促炎细胞因子的产生，支持了其转化相关性。总之，这些发现确定了微生物对 c-Rel 依赖性 IL-23 信号传导的调节是抑制肠-关节炎症的一种可行机制，并强调了靶向上游 NF-κB 通路作为 IL-23 驱动的免疫介导疾病的治疗策略。

## Abstract
The IL-23/Th17 axis is a central driver of intestinal and spondyloarthritic inflammation, yet upstream regulatory mechanisms linking microbial signals to IL-23 production remain incompletely defined. NF-kB signalling, particularly via the c-Rel subunit, is a critical transcriptional regulator of IL23A (p19), positioning c-Rel as a nodal checkpoint in mucosal inflammation. Here, we demonstrate that cell-free supernatant derived from Enterococcus faecalis AHG0090 (AHG0090-CS) suppresses c-Rel-dependent IL-23 signalling and attenuates inflammatory pathology across murine models of gut and joint disease. In the ZAP-70 mutant SKG model of spondyloarthritis and ileitis, AHG0090-CS significantly reduced weight loss, joint scores, and histological gut inflammation following curdlan challenge. In the Winnie model of spontaneous colitis, treatment similarly diminished inflammatory cytokine production. Mechanistically, AHG0090-CS reduced IL-23p19 mRNA and protein expression in intestinal tissue and lamina propria myeloid cells, accompanied by decreased nuclear c-Rel intensity. Suppression extended to downstream IL-23-associated cytokines including IL-17A, GM-CSF, MCP-1 and IL-6. In human peripheral blood mononuclear cells and macrophages, AHG0090-CS attenuated LPS-induced IL-23 and pro-inflammatory cytokine production, supporting translational relevance. Collectively, these findings identify microbial modulation of c-Rel-dependent IL-23 signalling as a tractable mechanism to restrain gut-joint inflammation and highlight targeting upstream NF-kB pathways as a therapeutic strategy in IL-23-driven immune-mediated disease.
