## 摘要
巨噬细胞通过吞噬作用吞没并降解病原体和细胞碎片。通常认为降解过程仅在吞噬体内部化和成熟后发生。在此，我们报道了在新生吞噬杯（PC）闭合前存在早期的 DNase 活性。利用荧光 DNase 传感器，我们发现在多种巨噬细胞类型中，吞噬杯形成时均会出现快速且普遍的 DNase 活性。我们进一步鉴定出负责该过程的酶为膜结合型 DNaseX，它在吞噬杯形成期间被组成性地招募至吞噬杯。研究发现 F-肌动蛋白（F-actin）聚合与吞噬杯中的 DNase 活性相关，这可能是通过促进 DNaseX 与固体 DNA 物质的物理接触实现的。在功能上，我们展示了巨噬细胞通过直接物理接触降解细菌生物膜内的胞外 DNA（eDNA），在不进行内部化的情况下清除 eDNA 结构。这些发现揭示了一种此前未被认识的、在巨噬细胞膜上运行的 DNA 降解机制，适用于降解巨噬细胞无法直接内部化的大块 eDNA 物质。

## Abstract
Macrophages engulf and degrade pathogens and cellular debris through phagocytosis. The degradation process was generally believed to occur only after phagosome internalization and maturation. Here, we report an early DNase activity at the nascent phagocytic cup (PC) prior to its closure. Using a fluorescent DNase sensor, we revealed rapid and ubiquitous DNase activity upon PC formation across various macrophage types. We further identified the responsible enzyme as the membrane-bound DNaseX, which is constitutively recruited to the PC during PC formation. F-actin polymerization was found to correlate with DNase activity in the PC, likely by promoting physical engagement of DNaseX with solid DNA materials. Functionally, we show that macrophages degrade extracellular DNA (eDNA) within bacterial biofilms through direct physical contact, clearing the eDNA structures without internalization. These findings reveal a previously unrecognized DNA degradation mechanism operating at the macrophage membrane, suitable for degrading bulky eDNA materials which cannot be directly internalized by macrophages.
